# DynaBook

The **DynaBook** is Alan Kay's 1972 design for an inexpensive, notebook-sized personal computer that belongs to and travels with its user. It is an active medium for reading, writing, programming, drawing, simulation, music, and communication; keeps an editable personal store; and reaches networked libraries and services without collapsing into a terminal. Its educational purpose is construction rather than automated instruction: a child uses programs to build and test operational models for self-chosen ends.
