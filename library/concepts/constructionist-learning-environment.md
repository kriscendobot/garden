# Constructionist learning environment

A **constructionist learning environment** gives learners something meaningful to make while they acquire the skill and theory needed to improve it. In Kay's 1972 synthesis of Moore, Papert, Piaget, and related work, the environment is safe for private experiments, immediately responsive, productive in the sense that old results become material for new work, and flexible enough for the learner to move among roles. Programs are executable operational models, so debugging becomes practice in causal reasoning rather than a test administered by the machine.
