# Topic: petnames

> Abstract: Petname systems separate one holder's private human-meaningful name from machine-secure keys and from labels proposed by owners or introducers. This topic covers the Electric Communities origin, Zooko Wilcox-O'Hearn's single-namespace trilemma, the key/nickname/proposed-name/petname taxonomy, trusted UI, introduction edges, and the limits of local naming. It is narrower than `identity`, which covers identifier roles generally, and `capability-security`, which covers authority-bearing references and delegation.
