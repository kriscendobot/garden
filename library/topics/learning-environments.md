Learning environments are responsive settings in which a learner constructs, tests, and revises meaningful models instead of receiving a fixed sequence of instruction. This topic collects designs that treat the learner as an active agent, preserve safe experimentation, create satisfying intermediate artifacts, and connect immediate feedback with timely human guidance.
