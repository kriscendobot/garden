Personal computing treats the computer as a medium owned, shaped, and carried by its user rather than as an institution's remote terminal or a fixed-purpose appliance. This topic collects the architectural, interface, economic, and cultural arguments that make local persistence, programmability, portability, readable presentation, and optional network services parts of one design.
