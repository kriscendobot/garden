Abstract: Replying to Jonathan Shapiro on the day of the earliest public petname post located here, Bill Frantz says Communities.com, formerly Electric Communities, is about to release software using the concepts. This is contemporary corroboration of implementation provenance, not a separate definition or an invention claim.

Frantz responds directly to Shapiro's attribution of the idea to Mark Miller and others at Electric Communities. His short reply promises directions for seeing the product's “true names” once the Communities.com software is released. The remainder addresses canonical line endings and binary-file hashing in Shapiro's content-addressed repository design, so its naming contribution is limited to that product-history statement.

Read together, Shapiro's post and Frantz's reply establish that the true-name, nickname, and local-petname vocabulary was publicly discussed in April 2000 while a descendant of Electric Communities was preparing software that used the concepts. They do not establish who first invented each detail or when the internal design began.
