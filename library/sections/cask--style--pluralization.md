Abstract: CASK's one-rule style guide: prefer regular English plurals over Latin or Greek forms to be more accessible to non-native English speakers. Both forms are valid English, but the regular forms are clearer. The canonical examples are indexes (not indices), matrixes (not matrices), vertexes (not vertices), and appendixes (not appendices). The rule applies to code (variable names, comments, documentation) and to prose alike. This is the design-corpus's standalone `style.md`; the same rule is restated in the repository-root `CONTRIBUTING.md` Spelling section.

## Pluralization

Prefer regular English plurals over Latin/Greek plurals to be more accessible to non-native English speakers. Both forms are valid English, but the regular forms are clearer:

| Prefer     | Avoid      |
|------------|------------|
| indexes    | indices    |
| matrixes   | matrices   |
| vertexes   | vertices   |
| appendixes | appendices |

This applies to code (variable names, comments, documentation) and prose.
