> Abstract: DID Core fixes the vocabulary that separates a DID, its subject and controller, a DID document, a DID URL, a method, and method-specific registries, then makes RFC 2119/8174 keywords normative.

The terminology prevents an identifier from being mistaken for a person, organization, device, or document: the DID subject is the entity identified, while a controller is the entity authorized to make changes to the DID document. Conforming documents, producers, consumers, resolvers, and methods have distinct obligations. Capitalized requirement words carry the RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 meanings.
