# Detector limits and human review

Turnitin's own guide says its report can misidentify human, AI-generated, and AI-paraphrased text and must not be the sole basis for adverse action. It also excludes or qualifies several forms, including short and non-prose writing. This is vendor guidance rather than a validation study, but it is a clear statement of the tool's intended evidentiary limit.

Use a detector result, if one is relevant at all, as a lead for a human review of sources, claims, drafts, and declared assistance. Do not use it to stigmatize a prose style. A careful technical document may be predictable because precision and parallel structure are appropriate.
