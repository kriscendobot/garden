# Detector heuristics are aggregate signals

GPTZero describes perplexity as the likelihood that a language model would choose the observed wording and burstiness as variation in that quantity across a document. It says both are only some features in its model. This documents a detector heuristic, not an independent linguistic finding and not advice for evasion.

Low variation can be a genuine revision issue when every paragraph has the same cadence, transition, and rhetorical job. Revise by giving each paragraph a distinct job and by choosing sentence length for the idea. Do not add arbitrary fragments, typos, slang, or punctuation variation to alter a score. Variation without communicative purpose is another form of formula.
