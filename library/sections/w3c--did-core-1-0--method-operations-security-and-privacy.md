> Abstract: DID methods define creation, read, update, deactivation, and recovery operations and are responsible for their security and privacy properties, including key rotation, revocation, recovery, persistence, correlation, and service privacy.

Core requires method specifications to define syntax and operations and to document security and privacy requirements. The broad considerations warn that a resolver, a controller, a service endpoint, and a human-readable identifier all introduce different trust and correlation surfaces. A DID document's cryptographic material and its services therefore need lifecycle discipline, not just syntactic validation.
