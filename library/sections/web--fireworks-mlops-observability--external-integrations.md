Fireworks documents two external MLOps and observability integrations: Weights & Biases for tracking fine-tuning experiments and visualizing training metrics, and MLflow Tracing for recording prompts, outputs, latency, and related application traces. The page is an integration directory, not a telemetry-schema guarantee: instrumentation setup, retention, credentials, and data-sharing behavior remain governed by the linked platform documentation and the operator's account policy.
