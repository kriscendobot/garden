> Abstract: The bridge adds artifact availability and provisioning obligations, with qualification spanning closure, retrieval, graph resolution, rollback, model-free checkout, substitutions, and incremental adoption.

Generated source, ambient caches, whole-workspace conversion, registry identity alone, and invisible rewrites fail the goal. Qualification covers forged evidence, malformed archives, credentials, offline use, substitutions, and hosted platforms.
