The athanor README: the user-facing manual for "personal LLM alchemy" — a TUI/CLI that discovers, downloads, runs, and switches between MLX and llama.cpp local models on Apple Silicon while keeping OpenAI-compatible endpoints live for downstream tools (pi-agent, editors). It documents the registry, stable per-model ports, MLX capability-vs-flavor routing, the process supervisor and its policies, observability caveats, and the pi-agent provider-sync (the boundary most relevant to the garden and to Endo's `endopi` design).
