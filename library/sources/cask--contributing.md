> Abstract: CASK's repository-root contribution guide, entirely about document hygiene. Markdown style: wrap prose at ~80 columns and begin every sentence on a new line so a one-sentence diff does not rewrap the whole paragraph; avoid honorifics/abbreviations at a line end (a period at a break reads as a sentence end); break long sentences at a clause boundary; prefer Markdown tables over ASCII columns and Mermaid fences over ASCII art. Spelling: prefer regular English plurals over Latin/Greek forms. No PR-process or commit-message rules are specified in this file.
