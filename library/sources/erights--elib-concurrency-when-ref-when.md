**References as Observables** (Layer 1 of the Four Layers of When) under ELib — the
lowest primitive of E's when-layering: treating an eventual reference as an
observable whose settlement (fulfilled to a near or far target, or broken) can be
watched. The When\* reactors (Layer 2), the when-catch shorthand (Layer 3), and
joining (Layer 4) all build on this observability. The upstream chapter is an
unwritten stub; this source records the layer's position and its lineage to
`@endo/eventual-send`'s promise-settlement observation.
