This 2022 Spritely paper turns the petname taxonomy into concrete user-interface designs. It distinguishes user-chosen petnames, introducer-supplied edge names, and contextual proposed names; then applies them to contact lists and browser chrome. Its distinctive contribution is operational: petnames sort ahead of shallow introduction paths, proposed names remain visibly provisional, and trusted browser UI prevents a page from forging the user's remembered relationship.
