# Probing the Topology of the Space of Tokens with Structured Prompts

> Abstract: This 2025 sequel reconstructs a model's token subspace from systematically collected response statistics, with a generic embedding theorem based on transversality and a validation against Llemma-7B's known embedding. It extends token-topology study toward closed models, but recovers only topology, requires demanding token-level sampling, and is not itself an AI-safety enforcement mechanism.
