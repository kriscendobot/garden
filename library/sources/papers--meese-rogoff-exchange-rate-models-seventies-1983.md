Abstract: Meese and Rogoff's classic out-of-sample test finds no durable forecasting gain for seventies exchange-rate structural models, autoregressions, a VAR, or the forward rate over a simple random walk. Its enduring contribution is an evaluation discipline rather than a blanket impossibility claim: compare the actual decision-time forecast with the simplest credible benchmark on repeated future observations, then withhold a directional-trading claim unless the improvement is stable across the relevant horizons and error measures.
