The awesome-ocap PetNames wiki summarizes the three naming properties, the key/nickname/petname distinction, and a useful primary-source reading list. Its claim that a petname system has all three properties is only accurate at the system level: several distinct kinds of names jointly cover the properties. The page does not show one global namespace with all three.
