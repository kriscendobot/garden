## Abstract

"The Opportunity for a Virus-Invulnerable Desktop", Combex's CapDesk-era threat-model and market framing. It argues that the most-publicized viruses are the *least* dangerous because they announce themselves, whereas the genuinely dangerous threat is the quiet dormant Trojan awaiting orders — illustrated with the GRC.com denial-of-service attack, in which the 474 silently-suborned computers were in far graver jeopardy than the victim of the visible attack. It positions the E platform and CapDesk as the solution extreme-security organizations need.
