Zooko Wilcox-O'Hearn's October 2001 essay is the primary source for the naming trilemma. It defines distributed, secure, and human-usable in terms of trust boundaries, attacker-resistant lookup correctness under a universal ownership policy, and people's ability to handle names. It expressly does not claim a proof and already identifies local petname translation as the layered workaround.
