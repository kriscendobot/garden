Abstract: Engle's ARCH paper makes one-step conditional variance a forecastable state driven by lagged squared shocks. It supplies the original volatility-model baseline that GARCH generalizes: an explicit variance recursion, finite-moment discipline, an LM diagnostic for residual scale dependence, likelihood estimation, and a changing-uncertainty empirical illustration. Its in-sample inflation result motivates, but does not itself establish, an out-of-sample financial forecast claim.
