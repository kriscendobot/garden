Abstract: The 2009-2012 cap-talk archive is the object-capability community's move onto the mainstream web platform. The load-bearing questions of the era are web-shaped: whether ACLs can secure a multi-user Web at all (Tyler Close's "ACLs don't", refuting the access-matrix equivalence a decade after Shapiro first argued it on this same list), how a browser can hold authority when a capability *is* a URL (the web-key / powerbox problem), and why CSRF, clickjacking, and click-fraud are all the confused deputy wearing web clothes. In parallel the JavaScript-subset systems that become SES and Endo (Cajita and the Caja lineage) take their place in a taxonomy of live ocap systems next to E, Joe-E, and the seL4/CapROS microkernels. This slice now sections all of 2009's standout threads, including the August-November managed-language, authentication, full-abstraction, CORS, guard, messaging, geolocation, and Native Client discussions. All of 2010-2012 remains deferred to further cycles.
