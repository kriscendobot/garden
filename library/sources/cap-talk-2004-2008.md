Abstract: The 2004-2008 cap-talk era moves from capability-model definition into deployable object-capability patterns. CapDesk and Polaris test dynamic least authority on ordinary desktop applications; Firefox IDN spoofing drives the petname UI; password capabilities become web-keys; and Waterken's `ref_send` brings eventual references to Java. The list also reopens hard boundaries: whether capability confinement controls hostile behavior, whether password capabilities survive copying and disclosure, whether wrappers can mediate an arbitrary interface, whether authority implies information flow, and whether hybrid ACL/capability applications reintroduce confused deputies. In March 2008 the participants explicitly inventory the pattern lineage (sealers, trademarks, caretakers, membranes, powerboxes, and eventual sends). This source index cross-links those workshop discussions to the Miller, Close, and Polaris papers that later give them a stable published shape.

## Published-paper cross-links

- [*The Structure of Authority*](papers--miller-tulloh-shapiro-structure-of-authority-2004.md) formalizes the dynamic-least-authority argument being disputed in the May 2004 threads.
- [*Polaris: Virus-Safe Computing for Windows XP* (2004)](papers--stiegler-karp-yee-miller-polaris-2004.md) and its [2006 CACM revision](papers--stiegler-polaris-cacm-2006.md) provide the stable report for the CapDesk/Polaris experiments and the shatter-attack fix discussed on-list.
- [*Capability Myths Demolished*](papers--miller-capability-myths-demolished-2003.md) provides the property vocabulary repeatedly tested by the definitional and confused-deputy threads.
- [*ACLs Don't*](papers--close-acls-dont-2009.md) is Close's later access-matrix formalization of the hybrid-system confused-deputy discussion and its web-key remedy.
- [*Concurrency Among Strangers*](papers--miller-tribble-shapiro-concurrency-among-strangers-2005.md) gives the eventual-send, partial-failure, and defensive-correctness context for `ref_send` and session-failure discussions.
