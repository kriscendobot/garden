Abstract: Moreira and Muir's *Volatility-Managed Portfolios* supplies the portfolio-construction payoff for the corpus's volatility-forecasting evidence. At each monthly rebalance it scales a factor's next-period exposure by inverse recent realized variance, normalizing unconditional volatility. Across equity factors and currency carry, the managed portfolios generally have higher Sharpe ratios and positive factor-relative alphas because volatility is predictable while expected returns do not rise proportionally when risk rises. The result is a risk-sizing rule, not a license to predict return direction: it must be implemented with information available at the rebalance, transaction costs, leverage limits, and an out-of-sample policy evaluation.
