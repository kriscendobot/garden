## Abstract

"A Choice of Giants" is Kris Kowal's canonical problem-statement essay published 22 February 2024. It names the surplus-of-giants dynamic (social media, search, AI, browsers, app stores feeding on users who lack alternatives), diagnoses the browser's failure as a user agent, extends the critique to app stores and web-application dependency sprawl, and proposes the "better dream": chat as a medium of distribution for confined, archivable, peer-sendable "weblets" that obtain capabilities through user-mediated dialogue rather than ambient authority. The essay is the canonical vocabulary source for the terms **user agent**, **weblet**, and **chat as a medium of distribution** as used in the Endo project's public-facing writing. It closes by linking to Endo as the technical answer.

The essay is the first in a two-part sequence; the follow-on page (https://kriskowal.com/endo/) describes the Familiar (Pet Daemon), capability-grant-through-dialogue, and the daemon's role in weblet confinement.
