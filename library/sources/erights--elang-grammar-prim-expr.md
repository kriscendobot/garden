The **Primitive Expressions** page of E's grammar chapter: the atomic expressions
with no parsing ambiguity (variable-name, literal, quasi-literal, URI-literal, list
and map constructors) on which the precedence-ordered expression grammar builds.
The URI-literal's scope-supplied `uriGetters` dereference is the E-language root of
Endo's no-ambient-authority IO.
