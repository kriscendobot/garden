## Abstract

The Combex Technology index page: the public hub for Combex Inc. (Marc Stiegler's company, with Mark S. Miller as CTO) and the page erights.org links to for CapDesk. It frames the E platform as "Cryptographic Capabilities for Distributed Smart Contracting" and inventories the CapDesk primary documents — "E and CapDesk: POLA for the Distributed Desktop", "The Opportunity for a Virus-Invulnerable Desktop", "Coming Products", "The DarpaBrowser", and screen shots — alongside external pointers to Marc Stiegler's narrated capability-security introductions and "E in a Walnut". It is the navigational entry point into the CapDesk corpus; the primary documents themselves are ingested separately.
