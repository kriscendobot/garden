The pricing reference page: the Workers for Platforms Paid-plan cost model. A $25/month subscription plus metered usage (20M requests, 60M CPU-ms, and 1000 scripts included, with +$0.30/M requests, +$0.02/M CPU-ms, and +$0.02/script overages; duration free), the inbound-only and single-request-per-dispatch-chain billing subtleties, and a worked $71.80/month example. Points at custom limits as the runaway-bill and denial-of-wallet guard.
