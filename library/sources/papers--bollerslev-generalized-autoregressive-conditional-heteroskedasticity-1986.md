Abstract: Bollerslev's original GARCH paper generalizes ARCH by letting conditional variance depend on both lagged squared innovations and lagged variance. It establishes the GARCH(p,q) state equation, stationarity and moment conditions, diagnostic and likelihood machinery, and an inflation-uncertainty example. For the financial-forecasting corpus, it is the foundational compact volatility baseline: useful for forecasting risk and sizing exposure, but to be selected only by a held-out, loss-specified comparison.
