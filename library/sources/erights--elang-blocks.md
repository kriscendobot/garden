The **Block Structure** chapter states one of E's defining choices: **E has no
statements, only expressions** — `if`/`while`/`for`/`try`/`switch` are all
value-yielding expressions. The page carries the framing plus a one-screen cheat
sheet of E's sequential control structures, the `def` forms (variables,
functions, objects, message-plumbing), and inheritance-by-delegation
(`def … extends …`).
