The primary erights.org **three-vat Granovetter introduction** narrative:
depositing a reference at a nonce with `provideFor`, withdrawing it with
`acceptFrom`, and preserving E-Order across three vats without trusting the
intermediary — all built out of pairwise two-vat CapTP messages. The E/Pluribus
ancestor of the OCapN [[three-party-handoff]], and (with its companion
`acceptFrom` page) the source of the route-list argument that grounds
[[relative-routing]].
