The **Expression Grammar** page of E's grammar chapter: the expression subset
presented in precedence order (later = tighter binding), with each construct box
labeled by associativity and carrying a Kernel-E expansion. It is the concrete
realization of the chapter's two-layer method (surface E is sugar, Kernel-E is
semantics) and the ancestor of E's operator-as-message convention.
