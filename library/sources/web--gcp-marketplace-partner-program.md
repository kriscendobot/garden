Google Cloud Marketplace's partner program publishes seven product categories: AI Agents, SaaS, Virtual Machine (Compute Engine images), Kubernetes Applications (GKE), Data Products (BigQuery Data Listings), Professional Services, and free Container Images. Partners enroll through the Google Cloud Partner Network, set up a Marketplace vendor account, and maintain a Payment Profile. Google bills the buyer through Cloud Console (one consolidated invoice covering both the partner's products and Google's infrastructure). Standard revenue share is 3%, dropping to 1.5% for private offers ≥$10M. SaaS products integrate via Cloud Commerce Partner Procurement API plus Service Control API; customer accounts are bound by storing the customer's Procurement account ID in the partner's database. Review timelines are roughly 2 weeks; pricing-model review specifically is 4 business days.
