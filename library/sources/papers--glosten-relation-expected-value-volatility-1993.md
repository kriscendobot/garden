Abstract: Glosten, Jagannathan, and Runkle introduce the leverage-aware GJR-GARCH conditional-variance recursion in an investigation of the conditional relation between market risk and expected return. The paper makes both the asymmetry and the identification assumptions inspectable: negative shocks can revise volatility differently from positive shocks, but a fitted variance state is not itself proof of return predictability. Its monthly evidence is an empirical comparison point that must not be generalized across horizons without fresh evaluation.
