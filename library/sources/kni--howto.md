"How to Write Kni": the graduated, worked-example tutorial for the language. It walks a beginner from a one-line story through the first branching choice, show-once options, labels and jumps, endings, then into stateful territory — variables, conditional options, condition-and-consequence operators, a full locked-door example, question/answer notation, random events, sequences, loops, and procedures — closing with a "write a three-room story with a locked door" exercise. Tutorial-shape and intentionally overlapping the reference MANUAL at a beginner altitude; the two source files serve different reader needs (walkthrough vs exhaustive rules).
