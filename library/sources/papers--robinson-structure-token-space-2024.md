# The structure of the token space for large language models

> Abstract: This 2024 paper introduces scalable local-dimension and Ricci-curvature estimators for LLM token embeddings and applies them to GPT-2, Llemma-7B, and Mistral-7B. It finds varying-dimensional strata and strongly negative curvature, then connects those observations to fluency, overfitting, inference uncertainty, and retraining instability while leaving causality and broad replication open.
