Abstract: The final Pipermail era moves capability discussion from foundational definitions to deployable JavaScript and cloud systems. The load-bearing threads connect directly to Endo's design surface: persistent authority graphs versus upgradeable data, return continuations as limited capabilities, Cap'n Proto's hostile-buffer boundary, `DeepFrozen` as an E-order optimization condition, WeakMap rights amplification, capability-aware module loading, Sandstorm's layered confinement, webkeys and macaroons, and path-sensitive revocation through membranes. The era closes in January 2016 with a still-unsettled question: references to one endpoint are not interchangeable when they arrived through different revocation and attenuation paths.

## Archive boundary and post-2016 gap

This closes the captured Mailman/Pipermail archive described in [`cap-talk-1998.md`](cap-talk-1998.md). The last available bundle is `2016-January.txt.gz`. The successor at [Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/g/cap-talk) is a JavaScript application and does not offer a public mbox endpoint usable by the ingest scripts. Searches for a cap-talk-specific mirror or downloadable export found none. An authorized member or administrator may be able to export through Google Takeout or Vault; obtaining such an export is the concrete prerequisite for a post-2016 ingest.
