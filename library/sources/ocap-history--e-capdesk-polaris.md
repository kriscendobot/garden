## Abstract

Survey of the E programming language, CapDesk, and Polaris as the canonical first wave of working object-capability implementations, with a focus on what each demonstrated technically and why none achieved commercial adoption. Intended as the factual anchor for the B2 essay's "thirty-year history of technical success and market failure" objection, grounded in named sources rather than unsourced assertions.
