The **Lexical Grammar** page of E's grammar chapter: the tokenizer-level catalog
of identifiers, keywords (those in use versus those reserved for the future),
operators, and other punctuation. The punctuation roles record E's distinctive
surface choices, notably `<-` (eventually, the eventual message send and ancestor
of `E()`), `=>` (maps-to), `::` (audited by), `:` (holds / URI), and `?`
(such-that).
