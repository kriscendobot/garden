## Abstract

"E and CapDesk: POLA for the Distributed Desktop", the core CapDesk primary document by Marc Stiegler and Mark S. Miller. It argues that conventional "Winix" (Windows and Unix) operating systems grant every launched application the full authority of the user — "candidly, madness" — and presents CapDesk, a capability-secure desktop built on the E platform, as the demonstration that POLA-disciplined computing can look and feel like Windows or KDE while granting authority only through ordinary user gestures (File Open, drag-and-drop) rather than passwords or authorization lists. This is the source the Polaris HP Labs paper cites for the PowerBox / installation-endowment / designation-as-authorization design.
