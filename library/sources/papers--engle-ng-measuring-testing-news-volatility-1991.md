Abstract: Engle and Ng turn asymmetric volatility modeling into a testable forecasting problem. Their News Impact Curve makes a model's response to a new shock visible; sign and size-bias diagnostics test that response; and a partially non-parametric ARCH benchmark exposes where a convenient parametric curve fails. In Japanese equity returns, leverage-aware EGARCH and GJR improve on symmetric GARCH, but their extreme-tail behavior and crash sensitivity prevent treating either as a universal winner.
