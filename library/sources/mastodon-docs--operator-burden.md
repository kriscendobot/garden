## Abstract

Survey of the burdens facing Mastodon instance operators: technical infrastructure maintenance, active content moderation using Mastodon's four-level individual-moderation and three-level domain-block systems, legal exposure for user-generated content across jurisdictions, and community governance pressure. Intended as the factual grounding for the B4 essay's honest treatment of what it means to run an O2 community hub. The "A Choice of Giants" essay's identification of Mastodon's operator-trust problem is cross-referenced.
