Abstract: Giacomini and White provide the conditional, finite-window forecast-method extension of the pairwise forecast-comparison lineage. Instead of treating fitted forecasts as if their coefficients converge away, their tests retain finite-sample estimation uncertainty through fixed-sample or rolling-window methods, and test whether available forecast-origin information predicts which of two methods will have lower future loss. The unconditional test nests inside that framework. This closes the estimation-uncertainty axis alongside West, but it is not a many-model correction: Reality Check, SPA, and MCS remain necessary when a researcher searched a model or rule universe. Loss-agnosticism preserves the corpus's central boundary between conditional-volatility/risk forecasting and directional-return forecasting.
