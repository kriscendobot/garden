The **Pattern Grammar** page of E's grammar chapter: the pattern sub-language
whose forms match a specimen and bind names in the resulting scope. The namer
family (`varName : Guard`, bare `varName`, `& varName`, `_`) is E's only form of
variable definition, and the `: Guard` hook is the ancestor of `@endo/patterns`
and exo method-guard parameter shapes.
