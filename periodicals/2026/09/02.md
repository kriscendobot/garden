## garden-meta

### The cybernetics-audit remediation orchestration

The day's largest single effort. An orchestration job decomposed
`designs/cybernetics-audit.md` § 7 into seven parallel children plus one blocked
child, verified the board state live, and handed the campaign to the leader's
orchestrate timer
([the setup report](../../../jobs/tada/cybernetics-audit-orchestrate-setup.md)).
Three children landed real fixes on `main2`:

- **The budget-control loop**, recommendations 1, 2, and 3, as three ordered
  commits so no concurrent editor fought over the same scripts. Commit 1 makes the
  spend meter report "no signal" instead of a confident zero that had driven the
  worker count to maximum on no evidence; commit 2 gives the budget-leveling loop
  step-clamping, confirm-before-move dwell, a drain gate, and per-host active-kind
  steering; commit 3 stops the loop from actuating on setpoints the config
  disclaims and adds a measure-only weekly capacity calibration
  ([report](../../../jobs/tada/cybernetics-rec123-budget-loop.md)).
- **The inode-exhaustion loop**, wiring the state-clone keeper to the same free-inode
  measurement the root-repo guard already computes, tightening its sweep under
  pressure without bypassing its liveness guards, and making the previously silent
  unparseable-`df` branch page after repeated blindness
  ([report](../../../jobs/tada/cybernetics-rec5-inode-loop.md)).
- **The deploy-sensor alert**, so the deliberate-deploy sensing loop's own
  blindness (unreadable fetch, unresolvable sha, no deployed sha) is counted and
  raises one keyed alert in about an hour rather than going unnoticed for weeks,
  plus a per-host-class stalled-deploy fuse
  ([report](../../../jobs/tada/cybernetics-rec10-deploy-sensor-alert.md)).

The remaining four children (the fuzz-triage backpressure, write-side frontmatter
validation, scheduler admission, and inbox coalescing) failed, and the panel-error
retry child stayed blocked on a still-running diagnose job. The orchestration's own
closing record names the four failures and reports `complete-with-failures`
([orchestration record](../../../jobs/tada/cybernetics-audit-remediation.md)).
