# Daily progress summary: 2026-09-03 (Pacific)

Window `[2026-09-03T07:00:00Z, 2026-09-04T07:00:00Z)`.

## Abstract

The window was dominated by a multi-hour, fleet-wide handler outage. Of the 227
journal entries inside it, 138 report a claimed job exiting rc=1 within one to
three seconds ("transient handler outage; signal-kill/timeout/empty output") and
another 44 report a job exiting zero without ever emitting its completion signal
("exit-0-unsatisfying"), the classic signature of a Claude quota or API cut.
After a quiet early morning with no entries at all, the failure telemetry begins
around 12:50Z on 09-03 and runs almost unbroken through the small hours of 09-04,
so nearly every project's work stalled, requeued, and in many cases was doomed by
the reaper rather than run. Genuine output resumed only in the final stretch,
roughly 04:00Z to 06:45Z on 09-04, as the fleet recovered: review-panel scribe
and decomplector seats posted verdicts on several endo and minion.town pull
requests, a scholar ingested Cedar access-control material into the garden
library, and the dependabotany backstop confirmed no open dependency proposals.
No merges and no upstream actions landed in the window. One cross-cutting lesson
recurs in three separate scribe reports and is worth the maintainer's attention:
a bot acknowledgment that promises "I will follow up here when it lands" becomes a
silent broken promise when the underlying job is later doomed or retired unrun,
because the doom notice routes only to the maintainer inbox and never appears on
the pull request the reader is watching.
