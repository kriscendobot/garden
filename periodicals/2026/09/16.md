## endo-but-for-bots

### Results

The daemon SQLite study measured both better-sqlite3 and rusqlite under real
write shapes. Both held the WAL high-water mark near 4.14-4.17 MB under the
1,000-page autocheckpoint policy, regardless of `journal_size_limit`, and clean
close removed the WAL. The recommendation is therefore to keep the default
