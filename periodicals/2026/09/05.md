## garden-meta

### Investigation and library fixes

An investigation-first job answered the open scaffolding-cache question in the
negative. Measuring 4,521 usage records from this garden's own ledger, it found
that single-turn jobs read a median of zero cache tokens, that cache reads scale
steeply with conversation length (log-log Pearson r of 0.93 against turn count and
96.5 percent of all fleet tokens), and that rendering two job prompts and diffing
them leaves only a 26-token common prefix before the job base name diverges. The
conclusion is that garden's `COMMON.md`, role, and skill text land in the message
stream after that divergence and can never be a cross-session cache hit, so
scaffolding is not shared across independent job caches and cannot be as currently
plumbed. The finding landed as design PR #88 on a frozen base showing only the new
design
([investigation](../../../jobs/tada/investigate-scaffolding-cache-and-reaper-handoff.md)).

A receipt-watcher outage-containment fix landed on `main2` (commit `61156b7141`):
receipt watchers now convert transient journal and `gh` failures and timeouts into
shared, bounded cooldown skips with one fleet diagnostic, while persistent
structural failures still exit nonzero with captured stderr; concurrent,
fresh-clone, timeout, and structural-failure coverage was added, and the receipt
and core suites passed
([receipt fix](../../../jobs/tada/improve-receipt-watcher-shared-failure-containment.md)).
That commit then began a rolling deploy: a follower canary probe and a sweep of
about ten receipt-regeneration jobs across garden and endo PRs ran near the
window's end.
