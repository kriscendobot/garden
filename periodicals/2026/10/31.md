# Daily progress summary, 2026-10-31 (Pacific)

**Abstract.** Forty-eight journal entries sat over 986 board commits under `jobs/`, including 253 claims, 238 completions, 222 gauntlet records, and 178 direct posts. It was a heavy build day on endo-but-for-bots: new draft PRs #1588 through #1603 opened, covering confined HTTP fetch, Git workspaces for Fae and Lal, interactive Git remote provisioning, the Daemon-to-Manager rename, mount-based worker imports, the MVS resolver, and five Ironhorse test262 ratchet slices. Five PRs left draft through clean gauntlets: #1543, #1560, #1592, #1593, and #1597. Two more (#1589 and #1596) spent all six review rounds and were left for a human decision, and most of the rest halted on CI. The Ubuntu 26.04 runner image dominated those halts: the bwrap cleanup flake and the `asinh`/`acosh` oracle drift turned at least eight gauntlet stages red on code the PRs never touched. Several builders declined duplicate or superseded work instead of opening PRs. On minion.town, the move to hosted CI was refused again for billing and rescheduled. In the garden, twelve fixes landed on `main2`, mostly longer handler budgets and a cluster that bounds producer-lock holders after a 30-minute journal lock hold starved every producer.
