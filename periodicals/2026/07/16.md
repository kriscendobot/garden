# Daily progress summary: 2026-07-16 (Pacific)

## Abstract

A high-volume day of steady presses and heavy scholarship: roughly 204 jobs reached
`jobs/tada/` in the window, overwhelmingly on `endojs/endo-but-for-bots`, plus a
prolific financial-forecasting reading run for the `finbot` builder and a new
`package-json` library project. On the fork, the SturdyRef pass-style track ran a
