## xs-to-rust: a new engine-porting program (the headline)

The largest new thread of the window was the standing-up of **port-xs-to-rust**, a
program to reimplement XS (the JavaScript engine the `endor` daemon embeds) in
memory-safe Rust. It began from a confirmed fresh start (no prior lifecycle state,
no PR) and ran as a self-supervising lifecycle rather than a maintainer-driven one.

- **Design (Fable).** The supervisor posted `xs2rust-endor-design` on the Fable
  model per the program's policy; a peer gardener claimed it within seconds and
  produced `designs/xs2rust-endor-engine.md` (see
  `jobs/tada/xs2rust-endor-design.md`). The design is grounded in two primary-source
  research passes (XS and xsnap internals: the 245-opcode `XS_CODE_*` ISA, the
  metering increments and check points, the FourCC snapshot atom grammar, the xsbug
  XML protocol; and the Rust engine landscape: Boa, Nova, Kiesel, Brimstone). Its
  verdict is an oracle-locked transliteration of XS that keeps the same ISA, meter
  points, snapshot grammar, and debugger protocol, uses an index-arena heap and
  `forbid(unsafe_code)` engine crates, and pins C-XS as a permanent differential
  oracle in CI. Deliverable is a DRAFT PR against `llm`, kept draft, with an
  explicit no-upstream-interaction constraint. Open questions route to the
  supervisor, never the maintainer.
- **Build stages landed.** Stages 1, 2b (with heap, frames, and exceptions
  children), and 3-language all completed against `endojs/endo-but-for-bots@xs2rust-endor`
  (PR #600, kept DRAFT). The stage-3 language child landed four green increments
  covering chunk-backed CESU-8 string values, the `global` opcode and top-level
  `this`, increment/decrement/exponentiation, `let`/`const` closures, optional
  chaining and nullish coalescing, and `delete_property`. Its acceptance evidence
  is a differential dual-run against C-XS: `test262 language/expressions` went from
  155 to 418 covered with 0 divergent, the in-cargo covered-grammar bar stayed 0
  divergent, and the Miri GC suite passed with `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` intact
  (see `jobs/tada/xs2rust-endor-build-stage3-language.md`). Remaining opcode skips
  were kept as honest named skips owned by later children rather than forced.
- **Press cadence.** Ten `xs2rust-endor-press-*` jobs ran the supervisor's
  drive-the-next-stage cadence across the window, plus `xs2rust-endor-fix-stage1-review`
  and the `port-xs-to-rust-memory-safe-engine` supervisor jobs `s2` through `s5`.
- **Research feed.** A scholar cycle ingested `danfinlay/quickjs @ native-ses`
  under the XS-evolution lens (`scholar-ingest-quickjs-native-ses`,
  `entries/2026/07/03/061344Z-result-gardener-696dde.md`). Its characterization is
  directly relevant: that fork realizes the SES freeze half (`harden` as C
  deep-freeze, `lockdown` as intrinsic freezing, `Compartment` as a fresh context
  on a shared runtime) but omits the taming and permits half.

## Fleet self-hardening after the July 1 outage

About 41 `improve-*` jobs completed, most of them encoding the failure modes of the
prior day's quota outage. The root cause was recorded plainly in
`investigate-poisoned-garden-infra-jobs`
(`entries/2026/07/02/100639Z-result-gardener-4df0ff.md`): a sustained Claude
quota outage (07-01 00:26 to 00:50Z, recurring 07-02 01:20 to 01:45Z) made every
`claude -p` handler exit with a transient signature, and because the reaper counts
requeue cycles without distinguishing transient from deterministic, five requeue
cycles inside one outage poisoned five garden-infra jobs. Dozens of unrelated peers
failed identically in the same window, which is the proof the cause was
environmental. The same result flagged that the `endolinbot2` host identity was
drifted throughout (an operational defect routed to the maintainer inbox rather
than fixed autonomously).

The encodings clustered by surface:

- **Clone keeper self-heal.** A large cluster (roughly fifteen `improve-clone-keeper-*`
  jobs) taught the clone keeper to detect and re-provision a missing bare or tracked
  clone rather than wedge, with an escalation when it cannot.
- **CI rollup transient retries.** About six `improve-ci-rollup-*` jobs added
  transient-network and `gh` transient retries to the CI rollup path.
- **Gardener lifecycle.** `improve-gardener-transient-failure-backoff-and-fleet-brake`,
  the exit-0-unsatisfying journal gate and routine-requeue silencing, and the
  deadline-overrun collapse-double-journal and single-note refinements tightened
  the requeue and escalation behavior the outage exposed.
- **Scheduler and identity.** `improve-scheduler-preflight-validation`,
  `improve-set-schedule-preflight-validation`,
  `improve-scheduler-escalate-persistently-missing-preflight`,
  `improve-garden-identity-drift-detector`, and the mentor improve-job dedup pair.

`rework-poison-park-and-dedup` and `investigate-poisoned-garden-infra-jobs` closed
the loop on the poisoning itself.

## Review-retrospective double loop, designed and built

Both halves of a new self-correction mechanism landed on `main2`. The design
`designs/review-retrospective-loop.md`
(`entries/2026/07/03/055913Z-result-gardener-3eb31c.md`) specifies a double loop:
the comment watcher mints a second, deferred retrospective job for substantive
feedback that treats a review comment as an indictment of the review process, a new
`prosecutor` role discriminates a real review miss from new direction, misses are
stored and clustered under a taxonomy, and a floored threshold dispatches a
dual-goal improvement job. The builder job then made it live
(`entries/2026/07/03/062408Z-result-builder-587324.md`): a deterministic
`review-miss-record.sh` store with tests, the `prosecutor` role and
`review-retrospective` skill, and the comment-watcher mint gated on the verb class.
Stage 0 of the build restored `skills/self-improvement/SKILL.md`, a reference that
`roles/COMMON.md` and the mentor role had been pointing at after it was lost in the
v2 migration.

## Health

The board recovered cleanly from the July 1 outage and did not lose work: 144
completions against the day's posts, no jobs stuck in `doin` at window close, and
the poisoned garden-infra jobs re-posted with corrected bodies after the outage
