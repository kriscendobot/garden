# Daily progress summary: 2026-07-01

A fleet-under-load day rather than a merge-heavy one. Roughly 1,564 journal
commits and 104 job completions, but the visible texture of the window was churn:
a large `endojs/endo-but-for-bots` shepherd sweep across about 45 open PRs
collided with what looks like a systemic CI-rollup / rate-limit episode, and the
gardener lifecycle machinery spent much of the day requeueing and escalating
rather than landing merges. Thirty-two distinct jobs hit the exit-0-unsatisfying
requeue path (a handler that exits 0 without emitting its completion signal — the
signature of a claude usage or quota cut), twenty-six threw elapsed-constancy
"deterministic overrun misclassified as a blip" escalations to the gardener inbox,
and four shepherd jobs hit the full 2,400-second handler wall (rc=124). The
important counter-fact: **no shepherd work was lost** — every escalated and
overrun job (PRs #79, #242, #410, #420 among them) completed on a resume or
follow-up job, and the day's single largest thread was a run of fifteen-plus
garden-infra jobs hardening the fleet against exactly this failure mode. The
substantive completions underneath the churn were scholar's Cloudflare library
ingest (two new topics), the endo-but-for-bots #58 `makeExo` conversion, and the
`agoric-3-proposals` fork CI work. Only five completions journaled a full `result`
entry; the rest moved the board quietly.

## Fleet under load, and the self-hardening response (the headline)

The window reads as the fleet meeting its own limits and immediately encoding the
lessons. Around fifteen `improve-*` and `garden-*` jobs landed on `main2`, most
aimed squarely at the churn visible in this same window:

- **Deadline-overrun early escalation** (`improve-gardener-deadline-overrun-early-escalation`,
  commit `4fb807154`). A handler that hits its own wall-clock budget (rc=124,
  elapsed at the 2,400-second `GARDEN_HANDLER_TIMEOUT`) is now recognized as a
  deterministic overrun rather than a varying external kill, stamped with a
  distinct counter, and poisoned by the reaper after a lower threshold
  (`GARDEN_REAP_OVERRUN_THRESHOLD=2`) instead of the full five cycles. This is the
  reflexive detail of the day: the machinery landed in-window and its stamps
  (`<!-- garden-deadline-overrun -->` on PRs #79, #242, #410, #420) are visible in
  the window's own narration.
- **CI-watcher hardening for systemic outages.** A cluster —
  `improve-ci-watcher-detect-systemic-rollup-outage`,
  `improve-ci-watcher-backoff-on-rate-limit-cascade`,
  `improve-ci-watcher-surface-rollup-stderr`, and `improve-ci-rollup-surface-gh-error`
  — teaches the CI watcher to recognize a fleet-wide rollup outage and a rate-limit
  cascade and to back off and surface the underlying GitHub error rather than
  hammering. The names alone corroborate that a systemic CI episode drove the
  shepherd churn.
- **Identity and transient-error robustness.**
  `improve-gardener-assert-and-mark-identity-at-startup`,
  `improve-gardener-scaler-restart-identity-drifted-instances`,
  `improve-gh-api-transient-signature-connect-error`,
  `improve-mentor-transient-handler-exit-zero`, and the repo-watcher
  self-heal/render pair for a missing template.
- **The orchestration-job pattern** (`garden-build-orchestration-job-pattern-sequence-and-watch-children`,
  commit `95d646f62`, 14 files, +1,126). The maintainer's 2026-07-01 standing
  directive — for a multi-part job, always make one orchestration job that moves
  parked child sub-jobs into `todo/` in sequence or parallel and watches each to
  completion — is now a deterministic leader-only watcher (`orchestrate.sh`, no
  `claude -p`) built on the same promote-when-the-board-reaches-a-state substrate
  as `unblock.sh`, plus a new `orchestrator` role, an `orchestration` skill, and a
  design document. Encoded into CLAUDE.md and the liaison and gardener role files.
- **Governance encodings.** `garden-encode-directives-reliably-become-jobs`,
  `garden-encode-liaison-leader-watch-and-marker-raises-leader`,

## Scholar: Cloudflare Workers for Platforms and agent-payments

Four scholar cycles landed a coherent Cloudflare library arc:

- **Workers for Platforms**, ingested end to end across three jobs
  (`scholar-ingest-cloudflare-workers-for-platforms` plus the `-remainder` and
  `-references` follow-ons). A new vendor-agnostic topic `multi-tenant-platform`
  now indexes roughly 27 sections spanning the core model (dispatch namespace,
  dynamic dispatch worker, custom limits, outbound workers, tags, worker
  isolation), configuration (bindings, hostname routing, observability, static
  assets), get-started, and the four `reference/` pages, with four new concept
  pages.
- **Monetization Gateway and x402** (`scholar-ingest-cloudflare-x402-monetization-and-comment-open-payment-designs`),
  a two-phase cycle that ingested Cloudflare's Monetization Gateway announcement
  into a new `agent-payments` topic (x402 protocol, pay-per-request monetization,
  payment-as-credential) and then connected that material back to the fork's three
  open `@endo/gateway` payment designs, posting a design-specific comment on each:
  #396 (the `verifyPaymentProof` ledger seam), #356 (payment orthogonal to
  AWS-attunement), and #463 (an x402 `PaymentProcessor` adapter for the
  MCP-terminating Bridge). All comments are bot identity on the bot fork only.

One self-improvement lesson was routed to the liaison rather than landed directly:
Cloudflare and similar vendor documentation sites (Mintlify/Docusaurus-class)
serve a clean per-page markdown rendering by appending `index.md` or `.md` to any
page URL, and publish `llms.txt` / `llms-full.txt` indexes — a far cleaner ingest
input than scraping HTML, worth a one-line note in the library's "Sources from the
web" conventions.
