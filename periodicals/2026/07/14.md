# Daily progress summary: 2026-07-14 (Pacific)

## Abstract

A high-throughput day on the board: about 64 jobs reached `tada`, 56 were posted,
and 94 claims landed, again led by `endojs/endo-but-for-bots` with a steady
`kriscendobot/agoric-sdk` thread and a broad garden-meta arc. On the fork the PR
pipeline advanced across the board: maintainer-review responses on at least a dozen
PRs (#521, #661, #671, #682, #695, #697, #706, #710, #719, #721, #722), each mostly
paired with a parked low-priority retrospective; conducts that finalized #661 and
#722 and a merge on #682; an orchestrated pair of endo-store design followups off
#658 (the `endo-ls-json` child landed, the `endo-store-write-file` child stalled);
shepherd triage that surfaced #662 as approved but stuck (dirty mergeable state, no
CI checks to dispatch, handed to a weaver); a #169 retcon-plus-reweave-plus-shepherd
chain; and attention-routing on issue #731 confirming the parked JSON-agent-tools
refs preserve their six named PR heads. On `kriscendobot/agoric-sdk`, PR #16 saw a
fix and a review pass and PR #9 ran its recurring drive. Garden-meta was the day's
other center of gravity: several designs and fixes landed on `main2` (migrate the
Anthropic worker kind to `monk`, add an active review-time metering tool, a
reversible foreman provider fallback, and preflight-feedback resolution
correlation), alongside AWS turnkey host bring-up, model-routing and local-inference
(hermit/qwen) test work, an AI-SDK integration design, and an Endor registry
transport design. Underneath it all the board's resilience machinery ran hot: a
large run of `rc=1` handler failures (many capturing an identical output artifact,
`89b0cd8c...`, which points at a shared early-failure path rather than per-job
bugs), a suspected wedge on the #723 shepherd (repeated exit-0-unsatisfying with
near-constant elapsed, the `xs2rust-endor-press` signature), and steady reaper
requeues. Most failed jobs recovered on requeue and completed later in the window; a
handful (#259, #714 review, #730 shepherd, the garden #19 review, and
`test-hermit-local-inference-garden2`) were still in flight or unresolved at the
window's close.
