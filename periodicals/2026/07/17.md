# Daily progress summary: 2026-07-17 (Pacific)

## Abstract

Another high-throughput day dominated by `endojs/endo-but-for-bots`: about 194 jobs
reached `jobs/tada/` in the window. The headline story is the Rust XS port (PR #600,
`xs2rust-endor`), which drove all the way from stage-7 acceptance through a formal
STAGE-8 ACCEPTANCE and into stage 9, surviving three separate build halts (two

the npm-via-CAS registry proxy fixed a program-throw segfault and endowed `console`

### Rust XS port (PR #600, `xs2rust-endor`)

The dominant story of the day. The port entered the window at its stage-7 acceptance and
exited it mid stage-9, advanced by a chain of supervisors (s23 through s29) and hourly
Fable press ticks.

- Stage 7 was accepted and stage 8 dispatched as a six-child serial-halt orchestration
  (see `entries/2026/07/17/105407Z-progress-gardener-b0f6cc.md`). Stage 8 then halted
  three times in succession, each from a distinct non-spec cause: two transient API or
  usage-cap outage windows (roughly 11:30 to 12:40Z and 17:04 to 18:23Z) that killed the
  C-XS baseline child across five claims each, and one 2400s handler-budget overrun on an
  oversized boot-surface-remainder child. Supervisors s24, s25, and s26 recovered each
  halt in turn, re-cutting the remaining children as stage8b, stage8c, and stage8d
  orchestrations (`entries/2026/07/17/135439Z-progress-gardener-d70fc9.md`,
  `entries/2026/07/17/170300Z-progress-gardener-687a60.md`,
  `entries/2026/07/18/032725Z-progress-gardener-2df08a.md`,
  `entries/2026/07/18/034040Z-progress-gardener-s26.md`).
- Along the way the port cleared real engine surface: class-instance construction, the
  first whole-file-green daemon boot bundle (`polyfills.js`, via partial-descriptor
  completion on `defineProperty`), `String.raw` binding, and an `op_add` ToPrimitive
  trampoline, moving the workspace test count from 506 to 518 and the corpus from 1711 to
  1738 cases (`entries/2026/07/18/022141Z-progress-gardener-5d65d0.md`,
  `entries/2026/07/18/060705Z-progress-gardener-4000d0.md`).
- The s27 whole-stage-8 review surfaced an apparent `module_corpora` byte divergence
  (endor emitting one byte more than the oracle on top-level-await modules) and dispatched
  a fixer (`entries/2026/07/18/043000Z-progress-gardener-s27.md`). The s28 supervisor
  confirmed the fixer's finding that endor was correct: the "divergence" was an
  oracle-build artifact from a moddable source predating for-await-in-module-body, proven
  by 47/47 byte-identity against fresh oracles at both the committed and declared moddable
  pins. s28 then posted the formal STAGE-8 ACCEPTANCE and dispatched stage 9 as a six-child
  orchestration (`entries/2026/07/18/050000Z-progress-gardener-s28.md`).
- Stage 9 opened by landing the ToPrimitive child and the receiver-chain absent-key guard
  that greens `host_aliases.js`, then halted again at the boot-surface-close child on a
  deadline overrun after a partial push. The s29 supervisor recovered it into a stage9b
  orchestration of five children, which was the live state at window close
  (`entries/2026/07/18/062700Z-progress-s29-supervisor.md`). Completed orchestrations and
  supervisor jobs for the day are in `jobs/tada/` (`xs2rust-endor-build-stage8` through
  `-stage9`, `port-xs-to-rust-memory-safe-engine-s23` through `-s29`).
- The hourly press ticks kept the branch rebased onto `llm` (several clean force-pushes
  with lease, zero conflicts) and repeatedly deferred to live builder children rather than
  force-pushing under them, taking the wheel only when a chain genuinely stalled
  (`entries/2026/07/17/112721Z-progress-gardener-acc435.md`,
  `entries/2026/07/17/223457Z-progress-gardener-edfe79.md`). One press established a
  reusable gotcha now baked into the child specs: long scratch-worktree paths overflow the
  daemon's AF_UNIX socket path limit, so `test:rust` baselines must run from a short real
  path.
