# Daily progress summary: 2026-07-09 (Pacific)

## Abstract

A high-volume production day: about 138 jobs completed off the board, almost all
of them on `endojs/endo-but-for-bots`, where three big tracks moved in parallel.
The mount-extensions family that was decomposed out of the long-lived PR #127
became a fleet of fresh feature PRs (#650, #652, #653, #655, #656, #657, #658)
that were built, ran the gauntlet, and were shepherded toward green; the
endoclaw scheduled-execution timer advanced through its phases and its stack
(#617, #619) was restacked onto the grown head of #609; and a new module-loading
four-layer design landed as draft PR #659 and entered a round-two design panel.
Alongside the code, a long run of maintainer reviews on old and design PRs was
processed and then retrospected by the prosecutor, and every retro this window
came back as a dismissal (maintainer process directives and forward design
steering, never a review-process miss). Two friction stories ran underneath: a
wedged gauntlet child (the "xs2rust-endor-press" wedge) burned deadline budget on
PRs #653 and #661 until the reaper requeued them, and the garden's own triager
kept surfacing self-heal jobs against the `kriscendobot/minion.town` and
`kriscendobot/endo` and `kriscendobot/agoric-sdk` triager handlers, most of which
turned out to be already fixed on `main2`. On the garden-meta side two design and
hardening jobs landed on `main2` (tada token accounting, a triager
consecutive-failure circuit breaker), and two research questions filed as garden
issues (#34 content-addressing versus DID, #35 Noise IK versus XK) were answered.
Four scheduled dispatches fired: the esheets supervisor, the Pi release-watch,
the finbot progress increment (which rescued a stranded SES-compartments branch),
and yesterday's own periodical.
