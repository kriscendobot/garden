# Daily progress summary: 2026-07-06 (Pacific)

## Abstract

A dense, productive day across four programs. The board opened quiet (nothing moved
between midnight and roughly 04:00 Pacific), then ran hot from about 04:11 Pacific
(11:11 UTC) through the following UTC morning. Four bodies of work dominated. First,
the XS-to-Rust Endor engine port (endo-but-for-bots PR #600, still DRAFT) advanced
through three whole supervisor stages: it finished stage 4 despite a serial-orchestration
halt, caught and fixed two real memory-safety regressions (an infinite decoder loop
and an oracle-shim SIGSEGV), got stage 4 formally ACCEPTED, and dispatched stage 5,
the compiler port, which ran most of its seven children by day's end. Second, a scholar
drained the entire dialog-db ingest backlog into the cross-cutting library across
roughly a dozen cycles, then confirmed the repo unchanged and closed the campaign.
Third, a brand-new project, minion.town (an AWS OAuth deployment), stood up: a serial
then parallel orchestration carried its build phases from an MCP server through Google
and GitHub identity providers, an authorization policy, and a web gate. Fourth, the
garden invested heavily in its own reliability: a host-identity drift was flagged on
both hosts, the fleet scaled to twenty gardeners with a streamlined onboarding, fleet
transcript capture was designed, built, and armed, and a run of mentor-dispatched
script fixes landed. Alongside all of this the endo-but-for-bots fork saw a wave of PR
gauntlets, shepherds, daemon caplet builds, five new or refined designs, and a batch of
prosecutor review-retrospectives (all dismissals). The reaper stayed busy the whole day
requeuing deterministic handler overruns and a handful of exit-0 wedge suspects; those
wedges are the one recurring irritant worth an eventual maintainer glance.

### XS-to-Rust Endor engine port (PR #600, DRAFT)

The single largest program. It carried through three supervisor stage boundaries in one
day, with two genuine regression catches along the way.

**Stage 4 halt and recovery.** The stage-4 serial orchestration halted at child 5 of 8
(the modules child), which the reaper poison-parked after two 2400s deadline overruns
(`../../../entries/2026/07/06/114423Z-progress-gardener-ec1665.md`, and repeated overruns
at 12:59, 14:14, 15:50, 16:33, 17:23, 18:13 UTC). Supervisor `s9` diagnosed it in two
layers (`../../../entries/2026/07/06/184840Z-result-gardener-3fb851.md`): the modules
child was actually productive and had landed its full static-half scope, but a deeper
root cause made the whole workspace test suite non-terminating. An endor-fuzz test
(`decoder_never_panics_on_arbitrary_bytes`) entered an infinite loop, a real fuzz trophy
introduced by one of the stage-4a commits. `s9` retired the exhausted modules job and
re-cut the remainder as a fresh five-child orchestration (`xs2rust-endor-build-stage4b`),
leading with a new fixer to make the decoder total and fuel-bounded so the suite can never
wedge again.

**Two regressions fixed, stage 4 ACCEPTED.** The decoder-hang fixer landed and the
stage-4b children (async surface, compartment, lockdown/harden, ses-conformance) all
finished. Then supervisor `s10`'s whole-stage-4 acceptance review caught a second
regression (`../../../entries/2026/07/06/210935Z-progress-gardener-22045c.md`): an
oracle-shim SIGSEGV introduced by the lockdown-harden child, crashing the whole-tree
Function and Array dual-runs. A fixer traced it to a missing `mxPush(mxGlobal)` before
host-function installation, stamping garbage HOME pointers, one root cause behind both
the whole-tree crashes and the lockdown shim crash. Supervisor `s11` verified the fix
from a fresh checkout (172 of 172 workspace tests, no aborts on the Function, Array, and
Object trees) and formally ACCEPTED stage 4 on PR #600
(`../../../entries/2026/07/06/213541Z-progress-gardener-96ae01.md`).

**Stage 5 dispatched.** `s11` then dispatched stage 5, the compiler port, as a serial
halt-on-failure orchestration of seven Opus children (lexer, parser-expr, parser-stmt,
scoper, coder-expr, coder-decl, and a byte-identity stage bar). The stage bar is a design
kill criterion: byte-identical bytecode against the oracle compiler on the full
conformance corpus. By day's end the lexer, both parser children, the scoper, and
coder-expr had reached `tada`; coder-decl was still churning through repeated requeues
and wedge-suspect escalations
(`../../../entries/2026/07/07/012347Z-error-gardener-3a904d.md`,
`../../../entries/2026/07/07/024517Z-error-gardener-77f961.md`). The PR stays DRAFT by the
maintainer's finish-line directive.
