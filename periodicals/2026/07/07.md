# Daily progress summary: 2026-07-07 (Pacific)

## Abstract

One program dominated the day: the XS-to-Rust Endor engine port (endo-but-for-bots
PR #600, still DRAFT) drove seven straight supervisor stage transitions (s12 through
s18) grinding stage 5, the compiler port, toward its byte-identity bar. Each round a
supervisor reproduced the whole conformance measurement from a fresh checkout, named
every residual divergence to a concrete cause, confirmed the kill criterion was never
tripped, and dispatched the next fix orchestration. The residual fell monotonically
across the day, from sixty divergences plus twenty coder rejects down to a single
attributed file by the last review, and the closing arrow-capture fix landed so s18 is
parked to post the stage-5 acceptance. Alongside it, the minion.town AWS deployment
kept building out its login and payments surface (sign-in-with-Ethereum authorization,

### XS-to-Rust Endor engine port (PR #600, DRAFT)

The single largest program by far. Stage 5 (the compiler port, `endor-compile`) opened
the window with its seven build children finishing, but the `coder-decl` child was stuck

a dozen reaped stale claims across the two hosts before it landed). With the byte-identity
stage bar built, the review-and-fix cycle ran without pause across the rest of the day.

**Seven supervisor stage transitions, one per fix round.** Each supervisor independently
reproduced the entire conformance measurement from a fresh checkout against the pinned
oracle, named every residual divergence to a construct, declared the kill criterion not
tripped (zero unattributable divergences, monotone convergence), and dispatched the next
serial halt-on-failure fix orchestration:

- s12 reviewed the built stage 5 and found the bar not met: sixty divergent files (all
  one mechanical CESU-8 string-literal cause) plus twenty named coder rejects. Dispatched
  fix round 1 (`../../../entries/2026/07/07/102510Z-progress-gardener-a007a0.md`).
- s13 closed all sixty divergences and the twenty rejects on the curated corpora, but a
  broadened eight-subtree sweep exposed new classes the curated corpora never exercised,
  dominant among them NamedEvaluation not emitted for anonymous functions used as a
  destructuring default. Dispatched fix round 2
  (`../../../entries/2026/07/07/124612Z-progress-gardener-0d4a10.md`).
- s14 reproduced everything clean on the curated and module corpora but measured 118
  attributed residual divergences on the ten-subtree sweep, all named. Dispatched fix
  round 3 (`../../../entries/2026/07/07/162726Z-progress-gardener-045181.md`).
- s15 drove the object, assignment, and function subtrees byte-clean, cut
  `statements/class` from 113 to 62, newly measured `expressions/class` at 50, and traced
  roughly 57 of the 62 to one shared root cause: Endor needs a real
  instanceInit/constructorInit function scope for every field-bearing class. Dispatched
  fix round 4 (`../../../entries/2026/07/07/193116Z-progress-gardener-f4de57.md`).
- s16 verified the entire class surface byte-clean, then ran the first complete
  per-subtree enumeration of the language tree (120 compile-diff runs), which refuted the

- s17 (early on 07-08) reproduced fix round 5 and found the whole 20,602-file tree had

### erights text-reuse grant (issue #632)

@kriscendobot and the fleet a standing license to reuse and adapt or derive-from any of his
