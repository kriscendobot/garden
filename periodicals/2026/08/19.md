### PR #475 byte-array work

The continuing byte-array review produced both implementation and specification
work. Three identical `toIndexableUint8` helpers were consolidated in
`@endo/bytes`, while the genuinely different text-decoder helper stayed separate.
The getter-based genuine typed-array predicate was retained at the hardener and
pass-style sites because `ArrayBuffer.isView` would incorrectly admit `DataView`.
A separate verification established that modern XS can take the native immutable
ArrayBuffer path, then gated emulation-only tests so they do not encode Node's
current shim behavior as a universal engine invariant. See
[the helper consolidation](../../../jobs/tada/endojs-endo-but-for-bots-pr475-65999de6.md),
[the predicate decision](../../../jobs/tada/design-endo475-istypedarray-isview.md),
and [the native-path verification](../../../jobs/tada/endojs-endo-but-for-bots-pr475-verify-shimmed-claim-20260819.md).

The provider-side predicate proposal was also written for
`@endo/immutable-arraybuffer`: an `isEmulatedView` export grounded in the
provider's brand WeakMap, with category, spoof-resistance, native-immutable, and
silent-break tests. It was posted for maintainer decision rather than implemented
across packages. That work also exposed an automation defect where an acknowledgment
and a substantive reply share one marker namespace, allowing the acknowledgment
to suppress the later answer. See
[the predicate specification](../../../jobs/tada/endojs-endo-but-for-bots-pr475-spec-genuine-predicate-20260819.md).
