## Abstract

The window was one of the busiest on record, and its defining activity was a
fleet-wide **review-retrospective sweep**. Of 114 completions, 59 were
second-loop prosecutor retrospectives, and 92 fresh retrospective jobs were
posted, spanning nearly every open pull request across the projects. The sweep
was seeded by `refresh-pr-review-sequence-20260821`, which surveyed all 293 open
pull requests, recorded 100 merges since the start of the month, and rebuilt the
maintainer queue. In **endo-but-for-bots** the long PR #475 byte-array campaign

resolved, a reply-only audit answered a follow-up question, guard tests were
added, and PR #503 closed as superseded by it, while dozens of #475
retrospectives ran (a mix of held misses and dismissals). PR #796 extracted a
hardened `@endo/crc32` package, though its resumed gauntlet halted and a purist
panel requested changes. PR #398's rebase-and-conduct halted when a stale
approval and an exact-head guard defect blocked the merge. A new Endor Git
bindings build opened draft PR #4, and an upstream-master integration merged into
a PR and handed off its finalization. In **minion.town**, PRs #47 and #49 merged,
a whoami tool build passed its prover seat, and a weblet ocap redesign opened as
PR #51. Garden-meta shipped the OpenRouter provider design and wiring, a

board saw 430 transition commits: 165 posts and promotions, 132 claims, and 114
completions. The entry stream carried three project-tagged messages and many
result entries; most retrospective results land under garden-meta in the entry
partition because they carry no `project:` field, and a cluster carries a stray
