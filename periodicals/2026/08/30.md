**Fuzz and refusal handling.** The standing fuzz producer became a serialized

fan-out suppression, and corrupt disposable checkouts gained quarantine plus a
one-time rebuild. The handoff validator learned that staged gauntlets are valid
successors. Codex refusal handling now preserves the original refusal, skips a

candidate gate. See [repair serialization](../../../jobs/tada/improve-ironhorse-fuzz-serialize-standing-repairs.md),

installed an hourly guarded keeper, recovering the inode pool from about 1.1%
free to 62% free on this host. Completion now checks named handoff successors

successors. Error captures retain bounded head and tail slices instead of losing
the diagnostic tail. CI outage latches are serialized so concurrent watchers
emit one warning and one recovery. See
[the clone-leak repair](../../../jobs/tada/fix-inbox-journal-clone-inode-leak.md),
