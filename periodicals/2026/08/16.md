### Dependabot sweep and merges

The botanist fleet worked a full page of Dependabot PRs. Four merged into `llm`
after full diligence (base census, tag-to-commit pin verification, advisory scan
both directions, maturity floor, source read, CI green): `actions/checkout` 6.0.2
to 7.0.1 in #1001, `zizmorcore/zizmor-action` 0.5.3 to 0.6.2 in #1004,
`npm-packlist` 10 to 11 in #1009, and `electron` 42.5.0 to 43.3.0 in #1008. The

CVE-2026-70606 (a session cache reuse in `ProtocolResponse.url`) and the incoming
43.3.0 carries none, so the major bump is a strict reduction in exposure with no
consuming-code migration since electron is only the packaging toolchain there.

The grouped `all-minor-patch` #1005 drew a verdict of **embargo to
2026-08-21**. Its maturity floor came from a transitive `@earendil-works/pi-tui`
0.84.2, and its CI is genuinely red: a `@typescript-eslint` version skew the group
left in the lockfile (`rule-tester@8.66.0` nesting 8.66.0 while other transitives
float to 8.67.0) breaks `lint:types`. The fix is a manifest or resolutions
decision deliberately left for the recheck, so the embargo defers four moderate
advisories (a `ws` DoS and info-disclosure pair, an `esbuild` dev-server read, a
`js-yaml` parse DoS) by five days with no new exposure. A daily backstop sweep
(`dependabotany-recheck-...-170501`) then reconciled the whole ledger against live
GitHub and confirmed no open Dependabot PR remains, with #923 terminally closed.
One fleet defect surfaced along the way: the botanist auto-conduct bypass in

### The `@endo/claude` design (#995) merged; #997 halted

PR #995 (`designs/endo-claude.md`) completed its gauntlet and was conducted to
**merged** into `llm`, with a summary comment posted. Before merge the panel and a
follow-up design pass hardened the confinement model: the wire-watcher seat found
that DD7 stored the spawn's `--settings` and `--mcp-config` secrets under the same
ephemeral-state directory whose sibling is the live daemon socket that DD6 rests
its whole isolation boundary on; that the five-flag spawn refusal is presence-only,
so value-carrying `--tools Bash` or `--setting-sources user` re-open the surface
the empty values were meant to close (the `"alg": "none"` shape); and that the
version pin is checked on one PATH resolution of `claude` but a second is executed.
A maintainer inline comment from kriskowal asking to model an optional threaded or
follow-up session was addressed in `pr995-endo-claude-followup-design`, leaving the
PR mergeable.

The worker-retention research design #997
(`designs/worker-retention-and-revocation.md`) ran the opposite course: its
gauntlet **halted un-converged**, the panel-and-fix loop reaching fix round 6
without settling and stopping before a seventh panel. The rounds produced dense,
useful findings anyway (critic, decomplector, scribe, stylist, ergonomist seats):
a Thread 3 versus Q4 contradiction on whether `op:gc-exports` is authoritative
that hides a premature-collection hole, a `WorkerDiscipline` field whose placement
(in the content-addressed incarnation formula versus at request time) decides
whether changing discipline is a rename that strands edges, and a per-root lease
now overloaded to bound two unrelated intervals through one knob. Being a research
design with no build, it stays a draft.
