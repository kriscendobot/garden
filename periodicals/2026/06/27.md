## Garden infrastructure (the bulk of the window)

The fleet spent most of its energy making itself more reliable. Grouped:

- **Failure classification and self-healing.** A long run of `improve-gardener-*`
  and `self-heal-fix-*` jobs taught the fleet to treat signal-kills, offline
  return codes, empty-output-with-nonzero, and transient git-128 on claim as
  *transient → requeue* rather than fatal, and to fold report and return code
  into a single failure capture. Companion fixes hardened the follow-up handler
  (it no longer swallows inner `claude` or producer failures, and bounds its
  retry/quarantine of a wedged digest) and the deadmail path (timeout and surface
  post failures; verify a job landed before retiring it).
- **Deploy and units.** `improve-deploy-always-reconciles-units`,
  `deploy-sync-fleet-onto-landed-fixes`, retired-unit pruning, and
  `garden-deliberate-deploy-no-shared-tree-development` plus
  `garden-enforce-per-subagent-worktree` cemented the deliberate-deploy posture:
  the root checkout is a deployed version and all development happens in
  per-subagent worktrees off `origin/main2`.
- **Wedge recovery.** The watchman now resolves dirty-tree main2 wedges
  autonomously (`watchman-resolve-wedge-autonomously`); four concrete
  `resolve-wedge-*` jobs cleared during the window.
- **Producer and journal-landing robustness.** `garden-harden-producer-body-read-hang`
  (the inline-body-arg / stale-lock hang), `garden-fix-producer-arg-guard-uppercase-kind`,
  `land-journal-entry-hardening`, and `improve-scripted-journal2-content-edit-landing`
  hardened how entries and content edits reach `origin/journal2`.
- **Comment and classification.** `garden-fix-comment-classifier-misroutes-directive-to-rebase`
  and `comment-watcher-no-inactivity-anomaly` (drop the false "blind" alert and
  detect blindness with a positive self-test) corrected two misclassifications.
- **Foreman and proxy.** Token-quota backoff and metering from Claude Code session
  logs landed for the foreman; the proxy gained blocked-job parking with
  auto-unblock and auto-clearing of watchdog messages. `rename-killswitch-to-draining-marker`
  renamed the drain signal.
