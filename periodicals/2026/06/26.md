# Daily progress summary: 2026-06-26

A heavy build day. The new **finbot** project saw the most movement — nine
builds landed or branched, taking it from a v0.5 scaffold to real SES
compartments, an ensemble forecaster, multi-instrument portfolios, and a live
LLM-driven OODA loop. The **endo-but-for-bots** PR pipeline merged four PRs
(#507, #513, #545, #547), advanced #440, refreshed #277 on a maintainer ask, and
surfaced a premise-shift on #297 for a human decision rather than force-pushing.
Upstream **endo** work mirrored #3137 and opened a flatMapReader design/build.
Garden-meta encoded three conventions and cleared seven dead-letters. Meta-state:
the ~100-gardener fleet on `endolinbot` ran hot and mostly clean; two handler
errors (finbot-substrate-adapters, reaper-continue) were reaped and requeued and
deserve a glance.

## By project

### finbot (`kriscendobot/finbot`)

The day's center of gravity. Builds were promoted off the plan queue in waves and
claimed across the fleet:

- **SES compartments + real wallet** — replaced the in-process v0.5 capability
  attenuator with real SES `Compartment`s (ambient authority reduced to the empty
  set per role) and an `@endo/exo` Far wallet behind a revocable caretaker
  forwarder; added a process-boundary CapTP signing-worker reference. Left on
  `feat/ses-compartments` for review (security-surface change).
- **Richer ensemble forecasting** — grew the forecaster from an independent-walk
  GBM Monte Carlo into the ensemble engine the design called for, with a
  deterministic visual projection. Landed on `main` (`e9234ee`); full suite
  **225 passing, 0 failing**.
- **Loop / daemon wiring** — added a `compute` OODA hook to the harness loop with
  a `localOnly` / `jobBoard:false` dry-run path, plus `driver-compute.js` that
  warms a fresh simulator world per tick and asserts the wallet is never touched.
  Landed on `main`.
- **LLM role dispatch** — wired a real provider behind the `llm` hook and exposed
  the deterministic pipeline functions as agent tools. Landed on `main`.
- **Richer instrument models** — deepened the three instrument return shapes into
  realistic dynamics over a shared `stepInstrument` primitive. Landed on `main`.
- **Additional instruments** — carried the OODA cycle to multi-instrument,
  yield-bearing portfolios with correlation- and APR-aware scoring (dry-run only).
  On `feat/additional-instruments`.
- **Cyclical forecaster** — on `feat/cyclical-forecaster` (`2a8b0b5`), ready for
  fast-forward.
- **Forecast-evaluation fixtures** and **volatility-tolerance elicitation** also
  completed.
- *Needs a glance:* **finbot-substrate-adapters** failed in its handler and was
  reaped/requeued (the captured failure blob was empty — a capture defect worth
  noting); it was later promoted again and re-claimed.
