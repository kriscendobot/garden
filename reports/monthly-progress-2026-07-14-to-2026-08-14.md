# Garden monthly progress: 2026-07-14 through 2026-08-14

## Abstract

The month was dominated by delivery on [`endojs/endo-but-for-bots`](https://github.com/endojs/endo-but-for-bots). The Rust rewrite of the XS engine progressed from staged language and daemon-parity work to a retconned, rebased, green PR [#600](https://github.com/endojs/endo-but-for-bots/pull/600) and merged into `llm` on August 6. The follow-on Ironhorse campaign then turned a full 52,092-case Test262 measurement into an implementation program. Coverage rose from 4,740 cases in the August 8 baseline to 23,496 at the August 14 measurement, while the remaining 23,427 actionable cases were decomposed into causal work packages. In parallel, the fork landed or advanced content-location, VFS, SturdyRef, npm-over-CAS, immutable-byte-array, OCapN-over-Noise, and daemon tooling work. The periodicals for July 16-21 show the Rust engine and capability-oriented stacks as the sustained center of gravity ([periodicals/2026/07/16.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/periodicals/2026/07/16.md), [periodicals/2026/07/21.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/periodicals/2026/07/21.md)).

[`kriscendobot/minion.town`](https://github.com/kriscendobot/minion.town) moved from daemon and OCapN deployment seams to a live weblet gateway. Wildcard DNS, fail-closed on-demand TLS, origin-isolation headers, a CAS content plane, a powers plane, and publishing increments were built and exercised at the edge. A later security review found that the powers plane could resolve an untrusted reference against the gateway's top Host. A two-layer fix now rejects special names at publish time and host-shaped objects at serve time, but that fix was still awaiting the maintainer's production decision at the end of the window ([jobs/tada/minion-town-weblet-gateway-build.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/minion-town-weblet-gateway-build.md), [jobs/tada/minion-town-weblet-powers-host-escape-fix.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/minion-town-weblet-powers-host-escape-fix.md)).

Garden infrastructure changed almost as much as the projects. The fleet gained stronger root-repository and journal-worktree guards, safer per-job scholar staging, improved panel and orchestration checks, better quota classification, local and hosted model routing, and a five-part cost-and-capacity study. That study corrected a misleading model-cost ranking and found that maintainer review rounds dominate machine cost by roughly 50 to 190 times at the median. The operational counterpoint was sustained churn from quota refusals, deadline overruns, provider outages, and handlers that exited without the completion signal. This led the maintainer to pause all 23 recurring schedules at 2026-08-01T16:34:11Z because token spend was too fast.

That pause includes `daily-progress-summary`. Consequently, July 14-31 is synthesized from the existing daily periodicals, while August 1-14 is reconstructed from raw `entries/2026/08/01` through `entries/2026/08/14` and board transitions under `jobs/`. The absence of August periodicals is an accounting consequence of the schedule pause, not evidence of reduced development. Board history remained active throughout August and accelerated sharply again by August 12-14.

## test262

The Test262 effort changed from an aspirational parity bar into measured implementation work. The first authoritative full-tree report ran 52,092 cases against the pinned XS oracle. It found 4,740 covered cases, 38,400 unsupported cases, 19 Ironhorse failures, and one infrastructure failure. It also exposed three engine hangs and showed Proxy was wholly unimplemented at that baseline ([jobs/tada/ironhorse-full-test262-report-publish.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/ironhorse-full-test262-report-publish.md)).

The implementation campaign then completed bounded clusters for parsing, errors, object semantics, functions and classes, iteration, promises, modules, collections, binary data, RegExp, internationalization, resource management, realms, and Temporal. At the August 14 head, coverage had risen to 23,496 cases. The residual audit found 23,427 actionable cases and handed them to a 15-cluster serial orchestration rather than hiding them behind expectations or relabeling. PR [#970](https://github.com/endojs/endo-but-for-bots/pull/970) remained open and draft. The largest known residual families were unsupported opcodes, engine aborts, differing abort values, parse/decode failures, and non-primitive completions ([jobs/tada/ironhorse-js-25-temporal-integration.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/ironhorse-js-25-temporal-integration.md), [jobs/tada/ironhorse-js-26-residual-gap-closure.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/ironhorse-js-26-residual-gap-closure.md)).

At window end, the final report refresh had been promoted before the newly decomposed residual program had completed, while RegExp and `with` statement children were active. The main standing issue is therefore sequencing: finish the causal residual closure and rerun the authoritative report before treating the completion numbers as final.

### Cost, capacity, and operating posture

The five-part budget study corrected attempt accounting, added a host-attributed cost ledger and read side, joined machine cost to PRs, counted human review rounds, and proposed an omega-ranked deterministic promoter. The strongest result was that flat-subscription Anthropic work had been priced on the wrong denominator: after correction it became the cheapest measured paid arm, while Kimi and Fireworks were more expensive per proxy second. Across merged PRs, human review cost dominated machine cost by about 50-190 times at the median. This shifts optimization toward reducing maintainer review rounds and rate-limit waste rather than minimizing token-dollar estimates ([jobs/tada/garden-budget-ratecard.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/garden-budget-ratecard.md), [jobs/tada/garden-budget-triple.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/garden-budget-triple.md)). The proposed omega rank remained provisional because its source definition was not available, and no dispatch behavior changed ([jobs/tada/garden-budget-omega.md](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/journal2/jobs/tada/garden-budget-omega.md)).

The schedule pause was the immediate operational response to spend. It stopped automatic periodicals and all other active recurring schedules, but manual jobs, watcher-triggered work, orchestrations, and selected re-enabled checks continued. August's raw stream is thinner because fewer recurring jobs wrote entries, while board transitions show substantial delivery on August 5-14.
