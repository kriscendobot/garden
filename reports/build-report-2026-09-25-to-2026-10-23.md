# Garden build report, 2026-09-25 to 2026-10-23

Produced by the liaison on 2026-10-23 from `jobs/tada/2026/{09,10}`, `main2` history, and `gh pr list` merged-PR searches.

## At a glance
- **Completed jobs:** 5,815 reports landed in `tada/`. Most are pipeline steps, not products: gauntlet stages (1,292), the minion.town PR screener (268), canaries (198), reviews, and host health checkups (253 for oros alone).

- **Garden library:** 456 commits to `main2`, 341 of them fixes, 36 features, 25 docs and 12 designs.
- **Build and design jobs:** about 330 top-level jobs, most of them stages of the same builds.

## Garden infrastructure (`main2`)
- **Budget and fleet control.**
  - Standing per-subscription token-backoff ramp, plus a foreman-discretion pool design.
  - `retire-subscription.sh`, used to retire `claude-endolin2`.
  - `worker-derotate` for silent hosts.
  - A weekly accountant apportionment and a budget-request intake.
- **Gauntlet and review.**
  - Automatic producer handoff restored.
  - `--add-rounds` on resume.
  - An unaddressed must-fix summary on early termination.
  - PR-body template and concision checks.
  - A generated maintainer review docket.
  - Deterministic design-to-build handoff.
- **Safety.** A "Jev" pre-classification gate on foreign content before agent ingestion, covering the botanist, scholar ingest and sender-gated comments.
- **Minion.town proxy.** Screening and merging of delegated minion.town PRs (the 268 `screen` jobs), a containment-record preflight, and a review-queue clip generator.
- **Ironhorse automation.** A test262 ratchet gate, delegated ratchet merging, and a mentat watcher.
- **Reliability.** Repository-lock serialization with coalesced fetches, and date-sharded `tada/` storage. The 341 fixes are mostly hardening of watchers, locks, completion reporting, timeouts and the usage meter.
- **Other.** Skills for caplet validation, SVG visual review, and CI-runner switching; an OpenShell host pilot kit; handlers for Codex, Kimi and opencode.
