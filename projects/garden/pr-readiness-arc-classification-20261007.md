# PR readiness: arc and milestone classification (2026-10-07)

Child 1/3 of orchestration `pr-readiness-arc-plan-20261007` (job `pr-readiness-arc-classify-20261007`). It classifies the 115 open, non-draft, bot-authored PRs that the 2026-10-06 readiness audit found with no gauntlet staged. Arcs come from journal `config/arc-budgets/`. Milestones (M1–M11) are the journal `plan/milestones/` roadmap, matched through each PR's design record in `plan/README.md`. `disposition` follows the review decision of 2026-10-07: `gauntlet` for NONE or APPROVED, and `verify-changes` for CHANGES_REQUESTED. The `superseded?` column only flags a PR; nothing was closed. Children 2 and 3 act on this table.

Classification rule. A PR goes to the mandate thread it actually advances. A PR that serves none of the named threads (upstream SES or compartment-mapper hygiene, Familiar release, Chat UI, lint) goes to `unallocated`, not `endo-backlog`, because endo-backlog covers only gauntlets that are already staged. `garden-upkeep` gets fleet-health CI fixes (a red master, lint ceilings, leaking test daemons, host-environment hermeticity) and minion.town deploy-script hygiene, matching the infra/upkeep section of `projects/garden/review-priorities.md`.
