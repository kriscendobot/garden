# Dismissal: endo-but-for-bots #631 review 4650709899 (retro)

@erights (Mark Miller) reviewed the garden's DRAFT design PR #631 (thesis-translation
— a docs-only plan to publish a modernized edition of his own 2006 dissertation in the
fork's docs) with an empty review body and two inline comments on
`designs/thesis-translation.md`. Both are author decisions about the Ka-Ping Yee
figures, reducing to one preference: **do not degrade figure quality to avoid asking
permission.** He offered to grant permission for Yee figures in texts he co-authored
(with IANAL and an ask-Ping fallback), asked which Yee-credited diagrams were meant,
and said that if the redraws are clearly worse we should get the permissions we need
rather than reduce quality out of reluctance to ask.

Not a garden review-process miss. The design job deliberately surfaced
licensing/copyright as an open question TO the maintainer and gated publication on
recorded permission; the prior draft had adopted a conservative "redraw or describe,
never reproduce" rule specifically to avoid asking. This review is the rights holder
and maintainer-authority OVERRIDING that conservatism — expressing a quality-first
preference and disposing of a permission grant on his own co-authored material. A
rights holder's preference and grant are first-stated author decisions (taste, scope),
unanticipatable by any panel seat, gate, or standing instruction — the same class as
the sibling #631/02a749af dismissal and the #604/#288 maintainer-process dismissals.
The review process did not fail; it correctly flagged the question and the author
answered it with a preference. See comment_url for the verbatim review.
