## Judgment calls

- **Picked 60 minutes for the job timeout** (the dispatch's defensive option) rather than 45. The dispatch sketch named both as reasonable picks; 60 was named the more defensive one. Rationale: the cold-cache + 3-attempt retry budget alone is potentially 30 minutes; doubling that to 60 leaves the rest of the workflow (yarn install + build + npx playwright test) unconstrained, which matters when the Playwright CDN is slow enough to need the retries in the first place.

The reason I took it: with a 60-minute job ceiling and 3 retry attempts, a 10-minute per-attempt cap means one slow install attempt still fails-and-retries even when the job has plenty of overall budget. 15 minutes per attempt absorbs one slow run without retrying. Worst-case install at 3 x 15 = 45 fits inside the 60-minute job ceiling with 15 minutes of headroom for the rest of the workflow.
