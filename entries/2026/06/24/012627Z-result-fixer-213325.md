## Root cause of test failures

The `@endo/immutable-arraybuffer` shim produces **plain-object wrappers**
for frozen `Uint8Array` values backed by immutable `ArrayBuffer`s.
These wrappers have `Symbol.toStringTag === 'Object'` (not `'Uint8Array'`),
so AVA's concordance `deepEqual` treats mutable and frozen arrays as different
types.
Integer-indexed reads (`wrapper[i]`) return `undefined` on the wrapper,
causing silent zero-byte corruption in `encodeHex`, `compareBytes`,
`decodeAscii`, and `TypedArray.prototype.set`.
