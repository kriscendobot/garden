## Judgment calls

### Ask 3 (text codec): "correct me if I'm wrong"

The maintainer hypothesized that @endo/bytes can sense pseudo-typed-arrays and
compensate without an intrinsic install. Verified: bytesFromText and bytesToText
already reach @endo/bytes's CapturedTextEncoder / CapturedTextDecoder from
install-helpers.js, which capture the realm's originals at module load. That
capture is the load-bearing protection against compartment endowment override.
Each eval twin of @endo/bytes captures its own instance, but TextEncoder /
TextDecoder are stateless by spec, so captured instances produce identical
bytes for identical input. No cross-twin rendezvous on the intrinsic is needed.

Dropped the install modules (install-from-string.js, install-to-string.js) and
the corresponding permits. from-string.js and to-string.js now instantiate
CapturedTextEncoder / CapturedTextDecoder in module-local variables. Public
API of @endo/bytes is unchanged; tests refactored to exercise the exported
callables directly.

### Ask 4 (brand-check): "do we also need..."

The maintainer asked whether a brand-check spackle method is needed to
distinguish immutable from emulated-immutable ArrayBuffer. Verdict: not needed.

Two existing brand-check entry points cover the use cases:

- **Internal (freezable TypedArray constructor):** uses the hiddenBuffers
  WeakMap shared with @endo/immutable-arraybuffer via the narrow
  private-for-bytes.js subpath. Private to those two packages, survives
  lockdown. Not exposed; not a candidate for a spackle.
- **External:** the isBufferImmutable free function exported from
  @endo/immutable-arraybuffer (function-shaped, not a method on the
  intrinsic); plus the standard-track ArrayBuffer.prototype.immutable getter
  installed by the shim. Neither requires a new install.

Rationale lands in DESIGN.md § Brand-checking the emulated immutable
ArrayBuffer.
