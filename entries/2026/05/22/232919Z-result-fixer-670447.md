# Fixer result: PR #317 (familiar-telemetry-crash-reporting) — 6 must-fix + 9 summary-fix addressed

One commit `96dfe7a0f` pushed to `design/familiar-telemetry` on `endojs/endo-but-for-bots` (PR #317).

Net diff: `designs/familiar-telemetry-crash-reporting.md` rewrite plus `designs/README.md` integration.

## Must-fix-loop (6 items, all addressed)

1. **`DiagnosticsUploader.help()`** added to the interface (Endo capability convention per project `CLAUDE.md` § Modules and exports).
2. **`preview` / `submit` token handshake** reshaped: `preview(bundleId)` now returns `{ bundle, previewToken }`;
   `submit(bundleId, previewToken)` consumes the token the same session issued.
   The design names the token's lifetime (session-scoped; invalidated by `discard()`).
3. **`familiar.log` path contradiction** resolved: both `familiar.log` and `endo.log` relocate to `<state>/familiar/familiar.log` and `<state>/familiar/endo.log` in Phase 1.
   § Storage and processing locality's claim that every Familiar diagnostic lives under `<state>/familiar/` is now true.
4. **`endo.log` cross-process boundary** named explicitly: the daemon owns and writes the file;
   the Electron shell reads it through a new `DiagnosticLogReader` capability (`readRecentLines(maxBytes): Promise<string>`) the daemon holds and exposes to the shell.
   Daemon retains exclusive ownership of `<state>/`;
   shell retains exclusive hold of the uploader.
5. **"Preview is the contract"** strengthened: the preview pane now shows the **endpoint URL** alongside the redacted body, so both body and destination are part of the user's per-act authorization.
   § Privacy guarantees and § Capture flow agree.
6. **`DiagnosticsBundle.kind: 'error-log'`** dropped from the enum.
   The enum now lists only `'crash-report' | 'usage-telemetry'`.
   Error-log content rides inside a crash-report bundle as `payload.errorLogSlice` (a redacted slice of `familiar.log` and `endo.log` content), not as a separate bundle kind.
