In `scripts/jobs/gardener.sh`, the transient rc=143/137 classification folds in the reaper cycle count but not the handler's elapsed wall-time, so a benign deploy-drain blip (killed at a varied elapsed near a known deploy) reads identically to a job that deterministically overruns and is SIGTERM-killed at a constant elapsed every cycle — the two are only distinguished after 5 poison cycles. Capture the handler start time (e.g. `SECONDS` before the `timeout …` call) and include `elapsed=<n>s` in the transient progress note printed at the rc=143/137 branch. A near-constant elapsed across requeue cycles is a positive signal of a deterministic overrun (or a fixed external bound) rather than a random restart, lettting a human or the watchman self-test surface a genuinely-stuck job early instead of waiting out the full poison threshold. Low-risk, read-only of timing state, no new board state.
