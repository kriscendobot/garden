`scripts/jobs/common.sh`'s `reclone_clone()` (~line 4302) currently classifies a re-clone failure as offline only via `_fetch_stderr_is_offline "$GARDEN_CLONE_STDERR"`, missing the `rc -eq 124 || rc -eq 137` wall-clock-timeout fallback that `sync_clone()` already applies at lines 6820 and 6838 for the identical failure shape (a `timeout`-killed clone/fetch with no captured stderr, since the transfer was still in flight). This caused `garden-comment-watcher@kriscendobot-garden` to FATAL/exit 1 (self-heal journal blob `00ed5d1f278aa0bd76a55cfa4cd3ec4e300d867f`: "clone ... timed out (>45s) on attempt 1" → "FATAL: clone ... failed") instead of cleanly skipping the tick with `GARDEN_OFFLINE_RC`, on what is documented elsewhere as "the commonest symptom under ~100-gardener contention." Fix: capture `bounded_clone`'s rc in `reclone_clone` and extend its offline check to `[ "$rc" -eq 124 ] || [ "$rc" -eq 137 ] || _fetch_stderr_is_offline "$GARDEN_CLONE_STDERR"`, mirroring `sync_clone`'s existing pattern exactly. Any caller of `ensure_clone`/`reclone_clone` (comment-watcher's verify clone, and others sharing this primitive) benefits.
