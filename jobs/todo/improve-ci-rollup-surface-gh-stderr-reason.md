Drop the `2>/dev/null` on the `gh_pr_view_retry` call at line 56 (the `json="$(gh_pr_view_retry "$pr" -R "$repo" --json state,statusCheckRollup 2>/dev/null)"` line). That redirect silences the whole command-substitution subshell's fd 2, which discards `gh_pr_view_retry`'s own diagnostic `log` WARN — the line that names the real cause (definitive 404/401/403/422 vs. a transient TLS/rate-limit blip, with gh's captured stderr). `gh_pr_view_retry` already captures gh's *raw* stderr into its own tempfile and never leaks it, so this redirect buys no noise suppression; it only erases the reason. Removing it lets the reason WARN flow to the handler's stderr, into `ci-watcher.sh`'s `$rerr` capture, so the recurring `#N rollup unreadable (rc=1)` WARNs (e.g. #612 here, and the #503/#313/#463/#286 history cited in this file's comments) become diagnosable — throttle vs. expired-auth vs. a closed/deleted-PR 404 race — instead of an opaque rc=1. Note the ordering interaction with `ci-watcher.sh:251` (`head -n1 "$rerr"`): `gh_pr_view_retry` emits its reason WARN *before* line 57's generic fallback log, so the reason line is the first line and survives the `head -n1`; if you want both lines preserved, widen that `head -n1` to the last non-empty line instead, but the single-script change above is sufficient to surface the cause.
