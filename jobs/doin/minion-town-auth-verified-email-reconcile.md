## Why (context)

GitHub + Google federate through Cognito (pool `us-west-1_mDaTgjr1m`) into an oauth2-proxy web login gate. During live sign-in testing, two things were **hot-patched on the box but are NOT in the repo** (config drift a redeploy would revert):
1. **email_verified:** oauth2-proxy rejected federated logins because Cognito issues `email_verified=false` for external/federated users — a **known Cognito limitation** it does not fix even with attribute mapping. Interim: `insecure_oidc_allow_unverified_email = true` was appended to `/etc/oauth2-proxy/oauth2-proxy.cfg` (live only).
2. **allowlist:** the maintainer's federated email `kriskowal@kriskowal.com` wasn't authorized; it was appended to `/etc/oauth2-proxy/allowed-emails.txt` (live only). Source of truth is `config/policy.json` → regenerated by `deploy/aws/scripts/gen-allowed-emails.sh`.

**Durable posture we want:** rely on GitHub/Google's own email verification, **enforced at the point closest to the source — the thunk** — so the gate can stay relaxed but *safe* (anything reaching it was verified upstream, not merely unchecked). Make that real and reconcile the drift into the repo.
