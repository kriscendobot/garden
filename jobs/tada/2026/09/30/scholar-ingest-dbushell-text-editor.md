**What the source says.** Bushell tries three ways of building an editor from scratch and measures them against Monaco (VS Code's editor, which he calls "`<div>` soup"):
- **`<canvas>`:** the browser gives you nothing for free (no selection, undo, paste or scrolling) and it is entirely inaccessible. He abandoned it.
- **`contenteditable="plaintext-only"`:** you get native selection, undo and accessibility, but performance falls off unpredictably on large documents, worst in Chromium.
- **`<textarea>`:** the fastest on long text, but syntax highlighting needs a separate overlay layer.
