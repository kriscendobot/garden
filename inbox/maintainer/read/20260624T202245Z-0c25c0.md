Weaver escalation on endo-but-for-bots #57 ("rebase on #475"): the rebase is a
premise conflict, not a mechanical one, so I aborted it and did not force-push.

What I found:
- #57 ("admit immutable ArrayBuffer through codecs") models a byteArray as an
  immutable ArrayBuffer. It adds byteArrayToHex / hexToByteArray /
  byteArrayToUint8Array / uint8ArrayToByteArray to @endo/pass-style and wires
  the marshal capdata / smallcaps / encode-passable / justin codecs to that
  ArrayBuffer model.
- #475 ("narrow byteArray to plain frozen Uint8Array") deliberately removes that
  representation: byteArray is now a plain frozen Uint8Array, raw immutable
  ArrayBuffers are rejected as byteArray, and toBytes/fromBytes were renamed to
  frozenBytes/thawnBytes.
- Rebasing #57's 4 commits onto #475's head (b2e712164) conflicts in
  packages/pass-style/src/byteArray.js (confirmCanBeValid: "candidate instanceof
  ArrayBuffer && candidate.immutable" vs #475's confirmCanBeByteArray Uint8Array
  check), packages/pass-style/package.json (#57 adds @endo/immutable-arraybuffer
  + @endo/utf8 vs #475's @endo/hex), and yarn.lock. #57's marshal codecs import
  byteArrayToHex/hexToByteArray from @endo/pass-style; those helpers do not exist
  on #475 and #57 defines them against the obsolete ArrayBuffer model.

Why I stopped: re-expressing #57's codec admission against the Uint8Array model
is a redesign (drop/replace the now-superseded helpers, retarget validation and
the byteArray.test.js / marshal byteArray.test.js fixtures at Uint8Array), which
the weaver does not do on the fly. This needs a fixer/builder pass or a
maintainer call on direction, not a force-pushed mechanical rebase that would
re-introduce the representation #475 just removed.

Recommend: dispatch a fixer/builder to port #57 onto #475's Uint8Array byteArray
model, or confirm whether #57's codec-admission core should instead be folded
into #475's line. #57 stays as-is on its current head (2c8c2bfb) until then; no
push was made.
