(a) Location/nav: the docs site is TypeDoc (`typedoc.json` `projectDocuments`, pages render at `/documents/<name>.html`; `yarn docs`; docs-only PRs ride `ci-docs.yml`). The thesis gets a `docs/thesis/` directory with an `index.md` registered as one projectDocument whose frontmatter declares the chapter files as children, category `Annex`, plus a `/thesis/` redirect stub installed by `scripts/posttypedoc.sh`. For a single paper like Distributed Confinement, the lighter shape is one `docs/<slug>.md` projectDocument (category `Annex`), no redirect needed; do not nest it under `docs/thesis/`, which the design reserves for the dissertation.

(b) Copyright: this is the load-bearing part. The thesis's title-page grant covers VERBATIM copies and CITED excerpts only; a verbatim-prose-with-code-substitutions edition is a derivative work the grant does not cover, so my design gates publication on Mark Miller's explicit recorded permission (he is `erights`, maintainer-authority on this fork; PR #631 asks him directly, and open question 1 covers the form of the record). Check what license statement "Distributed Confinement" itself carries (erights.org pages vary); unless it grants derivatives, your safe default is the same: either (i) verbatim-with-flagged-substitutions gated on his recorded permission on your PR, or (ii) an original exposition with limited cited quotation, which needs no permission. Do not assume a license; keep the PR draft until resolved.

(c) Flagging style (please align, it is the conventions page my phase-1 builder will ship at `docs/thesis/conventions.md`): every translated code block opens with a caption line like `*Translated from E (p. 41); original below.*` and preserves the original E in a collapsed `<details><summary>Original E</summary>` block; inline fragments translate in place with the original in translator's-notes footnotes; additive commentary uses blockquotes opening `**Translator's note (2026):**`; dead links move to archive.org with the original URL in the link title.
