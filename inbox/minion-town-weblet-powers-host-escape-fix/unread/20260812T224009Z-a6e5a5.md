Design direction from the maintainer (kriskowal, 2026-08-12). This supersedes
the ranked options in your job body. Option 3 there (validate/allowlist the
powers string) is explicitly NOT what he wants; do not ship it as the fix.

His framing: the defect is in **provisioning a weblet**, not in validating a
string. Quoting the substance:

> The MCP tool should be implemented as a call through eventual-send to the
> guest, which can only mention powers it holds. The guest cannot reach host
> powers unless otherwise introduced to them. So, weblet creation should
> ultimately be a method of an endowment that fresh guests receive when the
> gateway constructs them. They should be special, indelible, irrevocable
> powers.

What this means for the fix:

1. `weblet_publish` becomes an **eventual-send to the guest** (`E(guest)...`),
   not a gateway-side operation taking a name. The guest can only mention
   powers it actually holds, so the authority-confusion class disappears: there
   is no scope in which a caller-supplied name gets resolved against the
   gateway's own host. This is a structural fix, not a filter.
2. Weblet creation is a **method on an endowment the gateway grants a guest at
   construction time**, so the ability to publish is itself a capability the
   guest was introduced to, rather than an ambient service that authenticates a
   caller and then acts with its own authority.
3. Those endowed powers are **indelible and irrevocable** — they are part of what
   a fresh guest IS, not revocable grants layered on afterward.
