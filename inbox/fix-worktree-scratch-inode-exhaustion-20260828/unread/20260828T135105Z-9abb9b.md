Sharpening from the maintainer: worktree teardown must not merely be
"standard practice" or a documented recommendation — it must be
AUTOMATIC on job finalization, unconditionally, regardless of whether the
job exited gracefully. A crash, OOM-kill, disk-full abort, or any other
ungraceful death must still result in the worktree being reclaimed — not
just the happy-path completion case. Design this as a mechanism (e.g. a
trap/cleanup keyed to the claim's lifecycle, or a reaper-side sweep that
doesn't depend on the job's own exit path running at all), not a
convention job authors are expected to follow. This replaces the earlier
"recommend whether this should be a standing practice" framing in the
original job body — it isn't optional, and the mechanism must survive
exactly the failure modes (crash mid-work, host resource exhaustion) that
caused today's incident.
