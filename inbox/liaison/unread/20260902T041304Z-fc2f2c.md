Self-improvement finding: ensure-project-worktree.sh is not safe against two concurrent invocations for the same job base. A second invocation saw the first checkout before registration completed and ran scratch_cleanup while the first was provisioning dependencies, leaving a prunable registration and a directory containing only node_modules. Consider a per-target lock spanning cleanup, worktree add, identity pinning, and dependency provisioning, or register/resume before provisioning. I recovered by stopping the duplicate and running the helper once to completion.
