# Proposal: botanist should define the maturity floor on the freshest MOVED TRANSITIVE version, not the headline

From the botany review of
<https://github.com/endojs/endo-but-for-bots/pull/868> (job
`endojs-endo-but-for-bots-pr868-dependabot`, 2026-07-28).

`roles/botanist/AGENT.md` § Posture says: "If the upgrade does not close a
known CVE in the consumed package, wait one week from **the upstream publish
date**." Read literally that is the headline package's publish date. The gate
in § Autonomous disposition separately requires that the full transitive set be
benign with "no 24h-fresh or newly-introduced package left unexplained", but it
never says a fresh **transitive** version moves the floor.

The pull request <https://github.com/endojs/endo-but-for-bots/pull/868> is the case that makes the gap concrete and non-theoretical. The
headline `eslint-plugin-unicorn@72.0.0` published 2026-07-14, so on the literal
reading its floor had already passed on 2026-07-21 and the maturity leg would
have read as satisfied. But the lockfile moved 27 versions, two of which were
**under 6 hours old** when dependabot opened the pull request
(`globals@17.8.0` at 3h 26m, `baseline-browser-mapping@2.11.4` at 4h 55m), with
three more inside the 7-day window. Those are exactly the versions the posture
paragraph names as the likeliest supply-chain vector, and they would have
sailed through on the headline reading.

I resolved it by taking the floor from the freshest moved version
(`globals@17.8.0`, floor 2026-08-02T16:39:39Z) and embargoing to that, which I
believe is what the posture intends. But I derived that rather than read it,
and the next botanist may derive the other way.

Suggested change, a clarification of the existing sentence rather than a new
rule: in § Posture, say the maturity floor is the publish instant of the
**freshest version that moved anywhere in the lockfile**, headline or
transitive, plus 7 days. § Autonomous disposition's one-shot recipe should say
the same, since it currently reads
`floor=$(date -u -d "<upstream-publish-ISO> + 7 days" ...)` with
"upstream publish instant of the headline upgrade" spelled out in the comment,
which actively points the other way.

Not landing this myself: per `skills/self-improvement/SKILL.md` § Notes from
the field (2026-07-03), a gardener does not land a role change unless the job
is a garden-infra build on `main2`, and rewriting an existing rule wants
explicit direction. Routing it here for the maintainer's call.
