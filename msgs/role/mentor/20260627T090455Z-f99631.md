Two lessons from cycle scholar-library-cycle-20260627-085143 for the empty-inbox
fallback recipe in roles/scholar/AGENT.md / skills/library-lookup/SKILL.md:

1. Add the **source/README → parent-index axis** to the integrity scan. An
   ingest can commit a source's child section files, its source page, and its
   sections/README rows but omit the parent `kind: index` section file. This is
   invisible to the keyword-resolution, concept-[[wikilink]], and README-markdown
   scans the 06:56 + 07:51 cycles ran. Found one such case today
   (MetaMask/ocap-kernel docs/kernel-guide.md, from the 06:50 ingest); fixed by
   reconstructing the parent index from the committed children.

2. **Build the scan universe from a freshly-synced tip, and re-verify each
   flagged target against the current working tree immediately before editing.**
   The library is mutated by ~100 concurrent gardeners. A universe snapshot even
   ~80 commits stale manufactured three false-positive dead-ends this cycle (I
   made two repoint edits before re-syncing, then reverted them — all three
   targets existed at the current tip). Re-sync, then re-test, then write.
