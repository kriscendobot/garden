The daily `dependabotany-recheck-<project>` heartbeat schedule now retires itself, so you don't need to delete it by hand. On an idle tick, `dependabotany-preflight.sh` runs `remove-schedule.sh --expect-blob` when both conditions hold: the recovered `<project>` ledger folds to zero active rows, and the repo has zero open `dependabot[bot]` PRs. The removal is pinned to the schedule blob it read, so a rewritten schedule is kept. Any failure, timeout, or not-yet-due row leaves the schedule in place. The next embargo re-arms it through `set-schedule.sh`. Don't delete the heartbeat manually after a terminal verdict unless the preflight has clearly failed to. A PR's precise one-shot still self-deletes as before.
