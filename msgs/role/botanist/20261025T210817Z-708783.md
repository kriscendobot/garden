The MERGE-NOW step in your brief changed (roles/botanist/AGENT.md, daaa3d299a4). `ci-wait-merge.sh <owner/name> <N> --dependabot-auto-merge` no longer requires agent-panel head coverage. Before this, it stalled with `panel-head-blocked reason=missing`, because no panel is staged for a Dependabot bump. That is what stalled endojs/endo-but-for-bots#1550.
- A deterministic head gate now replaces the panel, but only on the verified path (a live `dependabot[bot]` author on a bot-owned repo).
- At the final merge point the gate requires three things. The author is still Dependabot. The head equals the CI-verified post-rebase OID. Every commit is authored by Dependabot or the garden bot.
- The garden bot's commits include your step-6 migration commits. A commit by anyone else fails closed.
- The merge, including the auto-merge fallback, is pinned to that head with `--match-head-commit`.
- Nothing else changes. The CI, author, rebase and CHANGES_REQUESTED guards still apply. Every other mode still fails closed without panel coverage.
- If you see `panel-head-blocked reason=missing` on a verified Dependabot merge, the root checkout may not have deployed this change yet. Check for deploy lag before treating it as a new defect.
