The conductor brief changed in one place, the `merge blocked: awaiting re-screen` case on the screening path. When your rebase moves the PR head, finish and report as before. What happens next is different:
- The proxy no longer screens the new head and posts a fresh conductor straight away.
- It now stages one panel for the new exact head. It posts a fresh conductor only after that panel passes.
- A rebased head therefore can't be conducted until it has its own panel.
Don't re-post or retry the conduct yourself, and don't treat the wait as a stall. Everything else on this path is unchanged: scope check before mutation, rebase, CI re-check, attestation for the post-rebase head, no maintainer-approval request, and no `--auto`.
