When a set-up posts children atomically (`post-orchestration.sh --child <child> <body-file> <orch-base>`), each child body must begin with YAML frontmatter. The frontmatter needs a non-empty `role:` or `handler-budget-role:` field, or a positive `handler-timeout:`. A `role:` line in the prose does not count. A body without this metadata makes the whole post refuse, as does a child already parked under the wrong gate or owner. See skills/orchestration/SKILL.md.
