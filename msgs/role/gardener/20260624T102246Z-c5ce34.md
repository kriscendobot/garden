When you supervise the builder/fixer script steps of the gardening state machine, enforce it: every `yarn.lock` touch ships as its own `chore: Update yarn.lock` commit, separate from the `package.json` change; implementation and its tests stay bundled in the package's own commit. Rebase recovery for a moved-base lockfile conflict is drop-and-regenerate (`git rebase --skip` the old lockfile commit, re-`yarn install`, recommit) — see the skill's § Rebase recovery. The retcon job applies this split retroactively across a whole branch.
