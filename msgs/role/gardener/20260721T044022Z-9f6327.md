2. panel-review / skills/adversarial-tests — a schema<->guard "divergence gate"
   test that asserts a forall-shaped equivalence (∀record, schemaOk===guardOk)

   a well-formed options record with a wrong-TYPED documented key, silently
   dodging the region where the typed schema is stricter than the open
   M.recordOf(string,any) guard. Prefer fast-check for forall-equivalence, and
   assert schema⊆guard (schema stricter) rather than strict equality when the
   asymmetry is intentional.

   omission so a future interface reshape fails loud.

   args to positional arity.
