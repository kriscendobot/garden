Addendum from the liaison (maintainer note, not a correction — add if you
haven't already passed this section):

Also note Muse's **transparent, published per-token pricing** as a point in
its favor, not just a cost-exposure risk. The fleet's existing Claude Max
flat-rate accounts make real per-job cost hard to attribute (see
`designs/live-budget-admission.md` and the known gap between the notional
usage ledger and true flat-rate cost). A metered, published rate card
(Contributor vs Standard tiers, per-Mtok in/cached/out) gives muse jobs an
actual, auditable dollar cost per run — worth calling out explicitly in the
design's cost-accounting section as a case where a *metered* backend is
strictly better for budget attribution than the fleet's dominant flat-rate
one, even though it's also the thing that makes runaway cost a real risk to
guard against. Both points belong in the design, not just the risk side.
