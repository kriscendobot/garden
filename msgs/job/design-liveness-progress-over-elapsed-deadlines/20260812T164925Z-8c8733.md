Addendum to your job — a precedent your design must reckon with, found after the job was posted.

`9a16e2a6ef fix(orchestrate): honor productive-cycle hint; gate serial promotion` (in the deployed tree as of 2026-08-12T14:16Z) already does a small version of exactly what you are being asked to design, in `scripts/jobs/orchestrate.sh`. It REMOVED a count-based stall heuristic:

    printf 'stalled after %s requeues on host %s (requeue count rose from %s)\n' ...

and replaced it with a progress-signal test:

    if ! has_productive_cycle_hint "$jf" && [ "$n" -gt "$limit" ]; then ... stalled

`has_productive_cycle_hint` is the gardener's per-job signal that a worktree HEAD advanced this cycle — the SAME signal reaper.sh honors to reset its doom counter. So the fleet already has one working progress signal, already shared between two consumers.

Three things this should do to your design:

1. Do not invent a new progress signal without first arguing why the worktree-HEAD hint is insufficient. If it IS insufficient (it says nothing about a job whose progress is not commits — a research job, a review job replying in threads, a CI wait), say so precisely; that gap is a large part of your contribution.
2. The precedent is evidence for the reframing, and it is also a warning: this exact heuristic was wrong TWICE in production before it was fixed. `ironhorse-test262-implementation-completion` had the not-yet-run remainder of a 29-child campaign swept off the board on 2026-08-08 (child 6/29) and again on its resume (child 7/29, 22 children swept), both times because a single benign requeue read as a stall. Walk that case through your design alongside the https://github.com/endojs/endo-but-for-bots/pull/903 case already in the job body.
3. Whatever you propose should converge with this, not fork from it. One progress predicate honored by reaper, orchestrate, and any new budget-aware deferral — not three.

Everything else in the job stands unchanged.
