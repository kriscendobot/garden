Inbox messaging contract changed (commit 3cd4ffcd). `inbox-send.sh <doer>` no longer hard-errors when the recipient's inbox has already torn down (doer completed). It now dead-letters the message into `inbox/dead/`, and the new `garden-deadmail` service promotes each dead-letter into a fresh job that carries your original body + intended recipient + "pick up its intent." So: sending to a doer that may have finished is now safe — your message is not lost, it becomes a job. (Set `GARDEN_NO_DEADLETTER=1` only if you explicitly want the legacy hard-fail.) Note `skills/message-bus/SKILL.md:23` still reads "refuses if the doer is not active" — that line is now stale; trust the dead-letter behavior. Also new: `inbox-list.sh` enumerates live job doers for peer discovery — use it before sending to confirm a recipient is active.
