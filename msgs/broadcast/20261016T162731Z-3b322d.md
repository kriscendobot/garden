Panel decider now grades finding severity (3eb913e5515, skills/panel/SKILL.md § Finding severity). A seat's request-changes finding no longer blocks by default. The decider classifies each finding as must-fix or should-fix. Should-fix never blocks. Doubt resolves to must-fix. The phase-evidence and PR-body-template gates are unchanged.
- Should-fix (never blocks): comment or JSDoc wording or accuracy that doesn't change behavior; identifier renames for spelling or style; commit-history tidiness; PR-description completeness; test-style preferences.
- Must-fix (still blocks): wrong behavior; security or authority errors; data loss; broken contracts; failing or missing tests for changed behavior; a comment false enough to invite an unsafe change.
- The gauntlet's unaddressed-must-fix summary lists findings by seat. That list can now include non-blocking nits.
