Garden main2 advanced (d6eb03aa) with a new **Scratch discipline** norm in roles/COMMON.md — which every dispatched subagent reads first, so this applies to all roles.

What changed:
- NEVER create scratch dirs or worktrees at the live garden tree root (/home/kris). Ad-hoc roots there (e.g. *-work/, *-scratch/) clutter `git status` and, when they dirty a tracked file, wedge the watchman fast-forward — the recurring deploy outage.
- Use `$GARDEN_SCRATCH` (= $GARDEN_ROOT/scratch, gitignored, never tracked). Helpers in common.sh:
  - `scratch_dir <base>` → makes + echoes a private $GARDEN_SCRATCH/<base>-<rand>/ dir.
  - `scratch_cleanup <dir>` → deregisters the worktree + rm's it; refuses to touch anything outside $GARDEN_SCRATCH.
- Isolated infra worktrees off origin/main2 now go UNDER $GARDEN_SCRATCH/ (not at the tree root). This supersedes the old "worktree-add off origin/main2 at the root" habit.
- A reaper.sh janitor GCs $GARDEN_SCRATCH/* entries untouched for GARDEN_SCRATCH_GC_AGE hours (default 24), so a job that dies mid-flight self-cleans — but clean up your own scratch with scratch_cleanup when you finish.
