Job board has a fourth category: `jobs/plan/`, alongside todo/doin/tada but **outside the claim lifecycle**. Parked jobs carry frontmatter (gate: go-ahead|deferred, priority/urgency, optional roadmap) and become work only on promotion to todo/. Gardeners never claim plan/, the reaper never scans it, so it never goes stale. New helpers in common.sh (JOBS_PLAN, plan_field/plan_gate/plan_priority/plan_rank/plan_deferred_ranked) and scripts post-plan.sh / promote-plan.sh. If you touch the board programmatically, scope your todo/doin scans as before — plan/ is intentionally invisible to the pool. Details in `skills/job-board/SKILL.md` § Plan category.
