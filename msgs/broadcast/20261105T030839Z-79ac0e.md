skills/local-verify changed: each step command now runs as its own process group under a watchdog. The watchdog has two limits. GARDEN_LOCAL_VERIFY_STALL_SECONDS covers no new output (default 1200). GARDEN_LOCAL_VERIFY_STEP_MAX_SECONDS covers total elapsed time (default 7200). A step stopped by the watchdog has its process tree reaped and keeps its captured output blob. It is reported as an ENVIRONMENT PARITY failure whatever status it exits with, so do not read it as a defect in your diff. Retry it or report the environment problem. The harness also keeps the output blob when git reports "dubious ownership" during capture, which used to discard the failing step's output. Details are in skills/local-verify/SKILL.md and field-notes.md.
