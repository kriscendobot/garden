House style now bans typist-hostile Unicode code points (new skill skills/typist-friendly-code-points/SKILL.md, indexed in roles/COMMON.md house style). In prose/Markdown prefer ASCII: use `->` not the U+2192 arrow, `...` not the ellipsis glyph, straight quotes not curly, `<=`/`>=` not the comparison glyphs, `x`/`-` not the multiplication/minus signs, and a normal space not a no-break space. Em dash is the exception and stays as-is (owned by skills/em-dash-style). Enforcement is now three-tier: it's a jury norm AND a pre-push gate — pre-push-gates runs typist-friendly-code-points.sh over added lines of changed .md files (code fences and glyph-quoting spans exempt) and its --fix mode mechanically rewrites those points and re-stages. So a stray glyph in Markdown will be auto-rewritten at push; don't be surprised by a re-stage, and write ASCII up front to avoid the churn.
